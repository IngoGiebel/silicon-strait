### §3.1 — PHL formal diplomatic frame: One-China-Policy constancy under Marcos Jr. *(first-pass synthesis from q-A1 11:00 notes; prose-tune deferred to 21:00)*

The Philippine government has held a single formal position on Taiwan since the **1975-06-09 Joint Communiqué on the Establishment of Diplomatic Relations between the Republic of the Philippines and the People's Republic of China** (the "1975 Joint Communiqué") and the parallel termination of formal recognition of the Republic of China. The 1975 Joint Communiqué structure — Manila acknowledges the PRC government as "the sole legal government of China" and the PRC position that "Taiwan is an integral part of the territory of the People's Republic of China" — has been restated in essentially unchanged form by every subsequent Philippine administration through Marcos Jr. The contemporary institutional consequences are concrete: the Manila Economic and Cultural Office (MECO) in Taipei and the Taipei Economic and Cultural Office (TECO) in Manila handle de-facto bilateral relations, mirroring the JPN Japan-Taiwan Exchange Association model.

The Marcos Jr. administration formal frame was tested on **2025-08-11 → 2025-08-13** when President Marcos in a public address described Taiwan in *"physical geographic"* terms that the PRC side read as departing from the strict one-China formulation. The DFA (Department of Foreign Affairs) under Secretary Enrique Manalo and subsequently Secretary Theresa Lazaro re-stated the formal frame as "clear and unwavering" adherence to the one-China policy [Source #A1, #A3 — 11:00 notes], creating a **register-split** between the executive (Marcos) and the DFA (Manalo/Lazaro) that the PRC response itself respected by addressing Marcos and not Lazaro (see §3.5). This split is the substrate for new predicate candidate **P24** (executive-vs-DFA register split — every player has at least two registers; the inter-state response respects the register split).

### §3.2 — PHL operational frame: MDT + EDCA + Balikatan + Cagayan-Isabela geography *(first-pass synthesis from q-A2 11:00 notes; prose-tune deferred to 21:00)*

The Philippine operational frame on a Taiwan contingency rests on three architectural layers very different from the JPN substrate. **Layer 1: the 1951 Mutual Defense Treaty (MDT)** — a binding mutual-defense commitment that explicitly covers attacks on Philippine territory, including the South China Sea per Pompeo's 2019 reinterpretation and Blinken's 2021 + 2023 affirmations; this is structurally stronger than the Japan-US Treaty of Mutual Cooperation and Security because the MDT's mutual-defense clause is symmetric (each party defends the other) where the Japan-US Treaty assigns defense-of-Japan obligations to the US without a symmetric Japanese commitment to defend US territory. **Layer 2: the 2014 Enhanced Defense Cooperation Agreement (EDCA)** as expanded by the **2023-04-03 announcement of four additional EDCA sites** including **Camilo Osias Naval Base in Sta. Ana, Cagayan** and **Lal-lo Airport in Cagayan** — both facing the Bashi Channel toward Taiwan, materially within range of a Taiwan-contingency air-and-sea-lift operation [11:00 notes, q-A2]. **Layer 3: Balikatan 2026** (April-May 2026) which per Day-10 [Source #17 Reuters] included the JMSDF destroyer *Ikazuchi* transiting the Taiwan Strait southbound Yokosuka → Balikatan — a structural three-way US-PHL-JPN kinetic-trainer pattern that materially differs from the bilateral pattern of pre-2023 Balikatan iterations.

The cumulative effect is that PHL — like JPN — has assembled every operational pre-condition for a contingency-relevant role without yet having staked a parliamentary commitment to a Taiwan-contingency trigger doctrine. The Marcos Jr. administration has *not* offered an analogue to the Takaichi 2025-11-07 *存立危機事態* Diet-of-record statement; PHL's operational pre-positioning runs faster than its rhetorical pre-commitment — the inverse of the JPN pattern Day-10 documented.

Audit findings (verbatim from Nisaba):

- **CCG claim passes.** Aug-Dec-2024 mean = 48.0 ship-days (SD 12.90, n=5); Jan-May-2025 mean = 94.6 ship-days (SD 18.35, n=5); mean difference **+46.6 ship-days**, lift **+97.1%** (`94.6/48.0 − 1`), Welch t = 4.645, df = 7.18, **p = 0.00221**, 95% CI for the mean difference **[+22.99, +70.21]** — wholly positive. Mann-Kendall on the same 10-month CCG series: S = 35, τ = 0.778, **p = 0.00236** — statistically clear upward monotonic trend. Pettitt regime-change test: maximal change statistic K = 25, estimated split **after 2024-12 / before 2025-01**, **p ≈ 0.0661** — suggestive of the expected January-2025 regime shift, at-the-edge of conventional significance for n=10.

- **PCG/BFAR claim needs correction.** The supplied rows do **not** reproduce the AMTI-stated +47% lift. Treating November-2024 as a genuine operational zero (Indonesia port visit + domestic disaster relief + Second Thomas Shoal resupply + combat drills per AMTI's own attribution): Aug-Dec-2024 mean = 15.4 ship-days (SD 9.10, n=5), Jan-May-2025 mean = 30.2 ship-days (SD 12.44, n=5), mean difference +14.8, lift **+96.1%**, Welch t = 2.147, **p = 0.0671**, 95% CI **[-1.35, +30.95]** — directionally positive but CI crosses zero. Treating November-2024 as missing-non-comparable: lift drops to **+56.9%**, Welch t = 1.882, **p = 0.122**, CI **[-4.26, +26.16]** — still positive direction but weaker evidence. The AMTI-stated +47% would correspond to `30.2 / 20.5 − 1`, but the supplied late-2024 rows sum to 77 across five months (mean 15.4) or 77 across four nonzero months (mean 19.25), neither of which produces a 20.5 baseline.

- **Interactions linkage is plausible, not proven.** CCG vs PCG/BFAR monthly Pearson r = 0.569 (p = 0.0858, CI [-0.094, 0.883]); total ship-days vs interaction days r = 0.580 (p = 0.0785, CI [-0.078, 0.886]). Both compatible with the qualitative claim that denser law-enforcement presence increases encounters, but n=10 is too short for stable effect estimates.

- **2025-09-16 USNI primary [Source #A15]** is *event-level evidence*, not a continuation row in the monthly series. The BRP Datu Gumbay Piang (MMOV 3014) bridge-damage water-cannon incident with PLAN-054 *Ma'anshan* "prompt live fire exercise" radio broadcast is suitable as a register-elevation marker for §3.5, not as a monthly patrol-days statistical observation.

**Bottom-line phrasing for §3.4 prose-tune.** CCG patrol-days went up materially and the level-shift is statistically clear; Philippine patrol-days went up directionally but the +47% AMTI headline does not survive the recompute, with the corrected lift either +96.1% (Nov-as-zero) or +56.9% (Nov-excluded) and CIs crossing zero in both specifications. **MOFA-PRC channel — P26 retraction.** The 14:00 fire's working candidate P26 (lexical-register country-class discriminator) rested on the claim that **MOFA-PRC spokesperson Guo Jiakun** had used the phrases "Those who play with fire will get burned" and "President Marcos needs to read more about the Taiwan question" at the **August 11-13, 2025** press conferences in response to Marcos's "physical geographic" remarks. The actual Aug-11-13, 2025 MOFA spokesperson was **Lin Jian** (also distinct from Guo Jiakun), whose transcripts in that window focus on the August 11 Huangyan Dao (Scarborough Shoal) water-cannon incident and contain **zero mentions** of Marcos, Taiwan, or "play with fire" formulations.

The Mao Ning 2024-01-16 canonical ZH original is verbatim [Source #A19, transcribed from `fmprc.gov.cn`]:

> 不要在台湾问题上玩火，切实恪守一个中国原则和中菲建交公报，立即停止在涉台问题上的错误言行，停止向"台独"分裂势力发出任何错误信号。

Inanna's lexical-register read of the original is load-bearing: the ZH text strictly uses **不要在台湾问题上玩火** ("do not play with fire on the Taiwan question") — the *advisory-imperative* register — and **explicitly does not** use **玩火者必自焚** ("those who play with fire will perish by it" / "will get burned"), which is the higher-tier register the PRC reserves for the US on Taiwan (the Xi-Biden November 2022 meeting formulation). The EN-secondary record's "Those who play with fire will get burned" rendering against PHL inflates the Chinese-language register by one tier, and the EN-secondary record's *Aug-2025* date attribution is **off by 19 months** against the canonical Jan-2024 primary. **P26 fails** on ZH-primary verification — neither the country-class discriminator claim nor its underlying register-inflation claim survives canonical-primary check on the substrate the 14:00 fire had hypothesised.

The function-class-vs-lexicon-class distinction is the load-bearing analytical refinement Day-13 contributes. ## §6 — Open questions for tomorrow

- **P28 third-substrate test selection: CAN vs AUS vs UK parliament.** The Day-14+ test of the substrate-discriminating Tier-3 floor predicate (P28: status-narrowing register requires constitutional-grounding + executive-independence + non-crisis-producing-deviation-capacity) is the load-bearing structural follow-up to Day-13. The Day-14+ task is to specify: (a) what counts as *constitutionally-grounded* legislative body for P28 purposes (a chamber elected with universal suffrage and constitutionally-protected role, or a stricter requirement including foreign-policy-mandate constitutional language); (b) what counts as *institutionally distant* from executive foreign policy (an oversight-only relationship without veto power, or a stricter requirement including the capacity to bind the executive on at least some foreign-policy questions); (c) what counts as *non-crisis-producing-deviation-capacity* (chamber-level votes that contradict the executive without legal consequence, or a stricter requirement including the capacity to take the deviation to a binding referendum or constitutional-court adjudication).

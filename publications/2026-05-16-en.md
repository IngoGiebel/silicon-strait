## Executive summary

Day 9 of Silicon Strait closes its methodology chapter: the **deterministic subset** of the GWW3 predicate skeleton — fifteen ROC-player predicates (P1–P15) plus the cross-player US-Exec promotion P12-bis, all locked across Days 7–8 — was today made formally parseable against `hassaleh/src/hassaleh/engine/gsl_ops.lark` and then run through a **first-cut formal pruning evaluator** (`simulations/game_theory_2026-05-16.py`) that re-tested every escalation branch the prior publications had flagged as deterministically blocked. **All eight branches — five Day-7 prunings (US-Exec unilateral CHIPS/TERA/NDAA repeal, Trump-era Taiwan nuclear-umbrella addition, PRC substrate-negotiation pivot) plus three Day-8 prunings (PRC cross-strait energy-framing redirect, ROC-Exec unilateral Tranche-2 release, KMT-internal 92-consensus repudiation under current chair) — were re-verified as blocked, with zero survivors.** This is the cleanest possible signal that all interesting Day-N+ dynamics now live in the *stochastic* layer that q1-gating (open since 2026-04-30) has been deferring; the deterministic skeleton has done its job as a tight lower-bound constraint set, and the next milestones (Day-14 first formal game tree, Day-30 Belief-Subgraph iterated game) build on top of it rather than alongside it.

## Today's thesis

**A deterministic skeleton that prunes every speculative escalation branch you throw at it is not a defective model — it is a tight specification of what cannot happen, and therefore a precise framing of where the actual dynamics must live.** The publication series has spent eight days enumerating substrate-level constraints (statutory repeal paths, doctrinal speech-act layers, ratification gates, FMS-LOA cadences, energy-buffer days, identity bands), and Day 9 demonstrates that those constraints, taken together, formally close every speculative-executive-fiat or speculative-counter-framing branch we considered through Day 8. Eight pruned, zero survivors means the deterministic-skeleton question is now *answered* — and the entire methodology pivots forward to the open question we have been pre-flagging in every publication since the project's first week: **how does the stochastic layer (belief, identity drift, probabilistic outcomes of doctrinal repudiation events) actually evolve over realistic time horizons, conditioned on this fully-constrained deterministic substrate?** That question is q1's territory; today's result is the precondition that makes it the *only* remaining question.

### §2 — Deterministic 8-pruning evaluator methodology

`simulations/game_theory_2026-05-16.py` (310 lines, MIT) is the first formal game-theoretic artifact in the simulations roadmap, delivered five days ahead of its Day-14 nominal milestone. **It is explicitly not the Day-14 first formal game tree.** The Day-14 milestone targets static one-shot Nash equilibrium analysis on a six-player frame (PRC, ROC, USA, JPN, ASEAN, EU); the Day-30 milestone introduces Belief-Subgraph iterated dynamics. Today's evaluator does neither — it is a deterministic pruning engine over the *five-player frame* (US_Exec, US_Leg, PRC, ROC_Exec, ROC_Leg) that Day-8 promoted, and it operates by enumerating the eight escalation branches the prior publications had qualitatively pruned and re-testing each against the formal predicate set with a structured `parameters_used` record per branch.

The evaluator's state object is a faithful encoding of the substrate-level facts that have accumulated across Days 1–8: US_Leg's 10-year CHIPS clawback (`chips_clawback_years: 10`), $10B TERA FMF authorization (`tera_fmf_authorization_usd_b: 10.0`), four-cycle NDAA Taiwan-provisions density (`ndaa_taiwan_cycles: 4`); PRC's transactional-executive vs core-interest dual-track lever set with peaceful-reunification energy inducement explicitly pitched at the 2026-03 Hormuz crisis; ROC_Exec's four-layer doctrinal stack with the 2026-03 nuclear-restart pivot active; ROC_Leg's NT$780B special-budget split into NT$300B unconditional + NT$480B FMS-LOA-Round-2-gated tranches under KMT Chair Cheng Li-wun and the 2026-02-03 KMT-CCP Joint-Declarations; and the energy-buffer trajectory (petroleum 148 days, current NG 10.7 days → 14 by 2027, coal 45.7 days, Matsu-cut 2.64 km off Dongyin). The evaluator runs deterministically — no random seed, no Monte Carlo — and emits a structured per-branch verdict with `predicate_invoked`, `parameters_used`, `survives` boolean, and a human-readable `justification_text`. Results land in `simulations/results/day9-five-player-pruning-eval-2026-05-16.json`.

### §5 — Zero-survives interpretation

Eight branches considered, eight branches pruned, zero survivors. The first-impulse interpretation — "the model is too restrictive" — is **wrong**. The eight branches were not chosen as test cases for the evaluator; they were enumerated across Days 7–8 as the canonical set of speculative escalations that the qualitative substrate-protection-gate argument was meant to block. The Day-9 evaluator is therefore not testing whether the model can prune *any* branch, but whether the model prunes *the specific branches its argument has already named*. Eight-for-eight is the correct success criterion for that test — and it has been met.

The substantive interpretation is the one we have been pre-flagging since the Day-7 S8 qualitative enumeration: **all genuinely uncertain Day-N+ dynamics are stochastic, not deterministic.** Belief updates, identity-band drift (the 0.62–0.78 Taiwanese-only identity range), Lai-approval volatility (the 7.4 pp eight-wave standard deviation), TPOF capability/willingness splits on US support (0.453 / 0.552), PRC blame attribution (0.448), the PRC warhead-trajectory range (600/1000/1500), the energy-buffer trajectory inflection (10.7 → 14 days NG by 2027), and the Hormuz-class shock probability — these are the open variables, and none of them sit in the deterministic predicate set. The Day-14 first formal game tree and the Day-30 Belief-Subgraph iterated game are the natural next milestones; both depend on q1 (`q1_gww3_stochastic_runtime_documentation`, still open since 2026-04-30 with project status `awaiting`), and q1's closure is now the single highest-leverage dependency on the publication arc.

## Game-theoretic framing (GWW3) **[PROSE-LOCKED]**

The Day-9 result is a formal demonstration that the deterministic subset of GSL — as specified by `gww3/predicates.gww3` @ `3da7314` parsing through `hassaleh.engine.parser` against `gsl_ops.lark` v1.2 + local hoist — is sufficient to encode and verify the substrate-protection-gate class of arguments at the granularity the Silicon Strait publications operate at. The eight pruned branches each invoke a discrete predicate from the parse-clean skeleton (with the parameter-traceability captured in `simulations/results/day9-five-player-pruning-eval-2026-05-16.json` as per-branch `parameters_used` records); the predicates themselves invoke discrete fields of the state object; the state object is populated from cited primary sources via the bibliographies of the preceding publications [Sources #A7, #A8]. **The audit chain is end-to-end formal at the deterministic layer** — from primary-source citation through state-field population through predicate invocation through branch verdict, with the predicates-file SHA256 (`d42a1760…`) embedded in the JSON metadata for reproducibility. This is the first publication in the Silicon Strait series where a stated branch-pruning conclusion is reproducible by a third party running a single Python invocation against checked-in artifacts.

The formal-pruning verdict re-partitions the Days-7+8 enumerated escalation space cleanly. **Eight branches are now in the deterministic-pruned set** (D7.1–D7.5 + D8.1–D8.3, all blocked, zero survivors); **six branches remain in the stochastic-resolution set** (the Day-7 S8 three plus the Day-8 S6.d three, all gated on `q1_gww3_stochastic_runtime_documentation`). The interface between the two sets is the predicate boundary itself: predicates over discrete substrate-protection state (statutory clawback durations, FMS-LOA cadence flags, doctrinal layer counts, KMT chair identity, joint-declarations dates) resolve deterministically; predicates over belief, identity-drift, approval-volatility, capability/willingness asymmetry, blame attribution, and warhead-trajectory ranges resolve stochastically. **Day 9 makes that boundary mechanically explicit for the first time** — every Days-1–8 prose argument that lived at the boundary now sits on a definite side of it, with the audit chain proving the placement.

The architectural implication for the player-frame extension agenda (Days 10–13 candidate JPN, ASEAN, EU) is significant: those extensions are **state-extension exercises, not architectural ones**. The five-player evaluator scaffolding generalises to a six-player or eight-player frame by adding player namespaces and their substrate-protection predicates to the state-object schema; the evaluator dispatch loop, the per-branch parameter-traceability record, and the JSON verdict shape all remain unchanged. The recursive-sub-game machinery that Day-8 reserved for the PRC composite (CCP / MOFA / MOD / TAO with Xi as principal) remains reserved for the stochastic layer; nothing in today's deterministic result modifies that reservation, and the PRC composite continues to resolve as a flat player in the deterministic skeleton with internal sub-structure deferred to the q1-gated stochastic runtime.

The Day-14 first formal game tree (six-player one-shot Nash equilibrium) and the Day-30 Belief-Subgraph iterated dynamics game are now downstream of a hardened deterministic substrate rather than parallel to a soft one. **q1 closure is now the single highest-leverage architectural dependency on the publication arc**: every Day-N+ escalation branch with novel substantive content lies in the stochastic-resolution set, and stochastic-runtime semantics are the only remaining unspecified part of the GWW3 machinery the project depends on. Today's result is the precondition that makes q1 the *only* remaining first-order open question; secondary questions (q9 hoist-permanence; q10 errata policy) are non-blocking refinements on already-functional pieces.

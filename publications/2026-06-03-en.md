## §1 — v1.2 textual-axis-pair methodology recap

Schema v1.2 promotion landed at Day-23 cron-E (commit `10381e9`, 366 lines, methodology track EN-only per q-day23-2). The promotion rests on a single load-bearing claim: the `coupling_textual` and `coupling_operational` properties of a substrate are *empirically off-diagonal* and therefore deserve separate stratification rather than the single-value `coupling` property that v1.1 carried. The off-diagonality criterion was operationalised as: 5 of 10 substrates (JPN, ROK, PHL, IND, UK) sit on the off-diagonal cell of the 2×2 (textual × operational) classification matrix [Source #2]; Cramér's V calculated on the populated cells exceeds the pre-committed threshold of 0.5; the textual-axis pair (broad ETF × TSM) and the operational-axis pair (firm × TSM) are sourced from structurally disjoint document classes — diplomatic-formal for textual, substantive-industrial for operational. Inanna's Day-24 cron-A source-validation confirmed all four sub-claims at the structural-coherence level (commit `d926630`).

A subtlety in v1.2's design becomes load-bearing today. The textual-axis pair was *defined* to capture "register-level" stress propagation: stress events that move TSM (the anchor) via supply-chain *signaling* — corporate guidance, government policy announcement, multilateral export-control updates — should propagate to substrate ETFs that index the substrate's broad equity exposure. The pre-registered prediction was that substrates with `coupling_textual=DIRECT` (only ROK has this token in v1.2 because of the inter-Korean clause that constitutes a directly-named coupling vector via the DPRK semi-conductor industrial relationship) would exhibit *larger* δ than substrates with `coupling_textual=INDIRECT` (US, JPN, EU) during the same stress windows. This is the H24.1 ordering hypothesis: ROK > JPN > EU > US, with EU 1-permutation-equivalent below JPN given the ACTIVE_AGGREGATE-dilutes heuristic.

What v1.2 did *not* commit to was a direction for ROK's δ. The schema asserts ROK has a `DIRECT` textual coupling property; H24.1 asserts that this property translates to a *positive* δ during the three pre-registered stress windows (DUV 2023 export-control list update; LAI 2024 election outcome and inauguration; PRC 2025-10-10 port-fee announcement). The blind interval [+0.05, +0.10] for ROK was anchored on the Day-22 firm-level finding (TATAELXSI × TSM δ = +0.0253 for the India operational pair, scaled up by the coupling-property bonus on the inter-Korean clause) [Source #3]. As of cron-C this morning, that direction is empirically falsified for this window/method pairing: ROK's textual-axis δ is negative.

Methodologically, the rolling-60 Pearson on log-returns, with stress-vs-baseline median differencing, replicates the Day-21 §3.2 method [Source #4] and the Day-22 §4.2 method [Source #5] — both pre-committed before Day-24's data pull. The four substrate-anchor pairs (SPY/EWJ/EWY/EZU × TSM ADR on NYSE) are the canonical alignment for cross-substrate ordering tests; TPE-listed 2330.TW was considered as an alternative anchor but rejected at cron-A pre-registration because the NYSE close window aligns cleanly with SPY/EWJ/EWY/EZU US-trading-hour observations and avoids the time-zone confound that would arise from a TPE-anchored sweep against US-trading-hour-observed substrate ETFs.

### §4.4 — Per-substrate substantive readings

**US (SPY) δ = +0.0342, inside [−0.05, +0.05].** US is the only substrate whose δ lands inside its pre-registered interval. This is a clean methodological corroboration of the baseline calibration: the US-broad equity ETF, with its `coupling_textual=INDIRECT` and `autonomy=DORMANT` v1.2 labels, exhibits a modest positive δ during these stress windows — consistent with mild risk-on broad-market behavior in stress relief sub-periods (notably the 2023-02 post-DUV-shock recovery and the 2025-10-30 PRC-truce phase). The δ magnitude does not require a substrate-specific explanation; it is a market-microstructure baseline.

**JPN (EWJ) δ = +0.0547, marginal overshoot [0.00, +0.05].** JPN is +0.0047 above the upper bound — a 9% overshoot of a 0.05-wide interval. This is the smallest deviation in the corpus and the only one consistent in *sign* with prediction. The substantive reading: JPN's `coupling_textual=INDIRECT` token, combined with `autonomy=ACTIVE` (which entails active substrate-level policy participation in semiconductor-supply-chain stress propagation, e.g. via METI export-control coordination [Source #11]), produces a δ marginally above the baseline. The overshoot is small enough to be within measurement noise; the directional prediction *passes* qualitatively even though the harness logic flags it as outside-interval.

**ROK (EWY) δ = −0.0077, sign-flipped from [+0.05, +0.10].** This is the load-bearing falsification. ROK was predicted to exhibit the largest δ in the corpus on the strength of its `coupling_textual=DIRECT` label, which the v1.2 schema derives from the inter-Korean clause: the DPRK's semiconductor-equipment relationship with PRC (via Russian Federation transshipment intermediation that surged in 2024-2025 [Source #12]) is the explicit textual coupling vector between ROK and the TSM-anchored stress complex. The empirical reading is the *opposite* direction: ROK's TSM correlation *decreases* during the three pre-registered stress windows by 0.0077 percentage points. Two non-exclusive readings are available: (a) the inter-Korean clause produces a partial-substitution effect on Samsung Foundry's competitive positioning during TSM-anchored stress — when PRC fab capacity is squeezed by US export controls, Samsung's foundry order book firms up *independently* of TSM movements, weakening the rolling-correlation; (b) the broad ROK ETF (EWY) is too dilute to capture the firm-level coupling, and a Samsung-Foundry-specific operational pair (already planned for Day-25 at v1.2's operational-axis layer) would surface a different signal. Reading (a) is a schema-update path that would force a re-interpretation of `coupling_textual=DIRECT` as a *partial-substitution* signal rather than a *co-movement* signal; reading (b) is a measurement-quality path. Day-25 is the test fire for distinguishing them.

**EU (EZU) δ = +0.0796, overshoot [−0.03, +0.05].** This is the substantively surprising finding. The v1.2 schema's `autonomy=ACTIVE_AGGREGATE` label was *introduced* at Day-23 cron-E to capture the heterogeneous-aggregation-dilutes-signal heuristic for the 27-member EU substrate. The prediction interval [−0.03, +0.05] was deliberately wider than the JPN interval and centered slightly below zero to allow for the possibility of negative δ from aggregation dilution. The empirical reading is *positive and the largest in the corpus* — δ = +0.0796, exceeding the interval's upper bound by +0.0296 (59% overshoot of a 0.05-wide interval). Two readings: (a) ACTIVE_AGGREGATE *amplifies* rather than dilutes the textual signal because aggregated 27-member exposure spans more semiconductor-supply-chain channels than any single member would carry — ASML in the Netherlands, Infineon in Germany, STMicroelectronics in France-Italy [Source #13], plus the EU IRIS² and EU Chips Act policy bundle [Source #14]. The aggregation captures the cumulative textual exposure rather than averaging it down. (b) The EZU constituent weighting (large-cap concentration in financials and industrials) accidentally over-indexes on TSM-coupled sectors versus the schema's intended representation. Reading (a) suggests adding a new v1.2 state `ACTIVE_AGGREGATE_AMPLIFIED` to distinguish per-member dilution from cross-member co-movement (q-day24-3); reading (b) is again a measurement-quality flag and motivates a Day-25 component-level decomposition of EZU's TSM correlation.

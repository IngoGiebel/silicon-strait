### §1.1 — Day-3's three assumptions (2026-05-01, commit `651cb5c`)

- **A1 — Mutual vulnerability**: TSMC dominance at the bleeding edge of logic (5nm and below) creates symmetric economic exposure to a cross-strait disruption — US, EU, Japan, Korea, and PRC industrial bases all depend critically on Taiwan-fabricated logic, and that mutual exposure is itself a deterrent.

Under acute cross-strait stress, the two institutional tracks meet — and the gap becomes the test surface H21.1 was designed to probe.

## Today's thesis

A pre-registered falsification rule earns its keep on the day it kills the hypothesis its author found most elegant. The §2.2 rule was written on 2026-06-04 to discriminate "EU member states co-amplify under cross-strait stress" (path a, the reading-α feeder) from "EZU's overshoot is a measurement artifact" (path b). The observed tuple not only fails path (a)'s gate — it inverts the channel logic the prediction intervals encoded: the one EU member whose index weight is dominated by the semiconductor leverage point (ASML) is the one that *decouples* under stress, and the members whose TSM-coupling runs through diversified large-caps (banks, luxury, energy) are the ones that *tighten*. Stress-coupling between EU equities and TSM is carried by the **global risk channel, not the semiconductor supply-chain channel** — which is precisely what path (b) asserted EZU was measuring all along.

## §3 — The inversion finding (substantive surprise #3)

ASML's weight in EWN was live-verified at this fire: **26.45%** of fund holdings as of 2026-06-11 [Source #3] — slightly *above* the ~25% the pre-registration assumed, so the inversion cannot be explained by weight decay. A fund more than one-quarter ASML — TSM's EUV monopoly supplier, the single most concentrated semiconductor exposure available at EU member-state ETF level — exhibits the *lowest* stress-window coupling to TSM of the three members tested, and decoupled outright in two of three anchor windows (LAI +0.0135 pooled-dominant, PRC −0.1015).

Reading: under cross-strait stress, ASML's equity does not co-move with TSM as a supply-chain partner; it prices its *own* idiosyncratic exposure (export-control regime, order-book timing), partially *hedging* the relationship — while diversified bank/luxury/energy-heavy member indices inherit TSM correlation through the global risk-on/risk-off channel. The project's running schema lesson sharpens: **textual-axis δ at ETF level measures risk-channel breadth, not supply-chain coupling depth.** This is the third substantive surprise (after LAI-2024 ROK decoupling, Day-25; PRC-2025 strong recoupling, Day-25) and the strongest evidence yet for the measurement-quality reading of the whole Day-24 cross-substrate anomaly family.

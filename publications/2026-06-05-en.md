## §5 — Marginality and ±5 trading-day anchor sensitivity

Nisaba audit §5 [Source #2] executed a `{−5, 0, +5}^3 = 27`-case anchor-perturbation grid on the ROK × TSM pair under the same harness, holding everything else constant. The result:

| Statistic | Value |
|---|---:|
| Original ROK δ (anchors as pre-registered) | +0.0515007346 |

The marginality is asymmetric: ROK can drop below the lower interval bound but cannot exceed the upper, under realistic anchor perturbations. This is the appropriate publication-safe phrasing per Nisaba audit §5: "ROK becomes positive and barely in-interval under the pre-registered anchors, consistent with the Day-25 firm-level signal, but the in-interval status is not stable under small anchor shifts." The brittleness observation reinforces q-day26-2's default toward a "marginal-corroboration" methodology footnote.

A subtler observation from the perturbation grid: the asymmetry is driven primarily by DUV ±5 and LAI ±5 sensitivities, not PRC ±5. PRC −5 and PRC +5 both land inside the interval at `+0.0523` and `+0.0515` respectively. This calendar-asymmetry reading is consistent with H25.1's per-anchor finding (Day-25 §4.3) that PRC_2025 carries the entire pooled lift; the PRC anchor is robust to ±5 td perturbation because the signal is strong enough that small calendar shifts do not threaten the verdict's underlying contribution.

## Today's thesis

The decomposition logic that opened at Day-24 closes today at the only instrument that can settle it. ETF-level evidence could never separate "ASML decouples" from "the 73.55% non-ASML remainder of EWN nets to a low number" — so Day-28's path (b) verdict, however well-supported, left one substitution available to a supply-chain defender. H25.3 removes the dilution and asks the channel question at the firm: *does TSM's EUV-monopoly supplier co-move with TSM when the strait gets kinetic?* The answer is no — and not merely "weakly positive." The pooled δ is **negative**, the PRC_2025 kinetic-window leg is **−0.0311** and the LAI_2024 leg **−0.0856**, both below zero, exactly the sign structure the H_id interval predicted from EWN's −0.1015 PRC leg. A firm whose entire equity thesis is its monopoly position in TSM's supply chain prices its *own* exposure — export-control regime, order-book timing, end-demand cycle — under cross-strait stress, and partially hedges the TSM relationship rather than amplifying it. The supply-chain-coupling channel is rejected at the instrument built to detect it. The project's running schema lesson hardens from observation to standing rule: **textual-axis δ at ETF *or* firm level measures global-risk-channel breadth, not semiconductor-supply-chain depth.**

### §4.4 — Anchor-weighted vs equal-weighted

The pooled δ_median (+0.0546) implicitly weights the three anchors equally by trading-day count (123 stress td total: 41+41+41 with a 3-day left-edge truncation on DUV_2023). If anchors were weighted by *substantive importance* — for instance by the magnitude of the event itself (PRC port-fee announcement materially larger fiscal impact than DUV equipment FDPR expansion) — the lift would shift further positive. But this would constitute post-hoc re-weighting that violates the pre-committed median-pooling convention. The publication reports the pre-committed equal-weight median and surfaces the per-anchor heterogeneity in the table above; any re-weighting belongs in a Day-26+ pre-registration that explicitly commits to a weighting scheme before data is touched.

## §5 — Brittleness footnote (rolling-window sensitivity)

The pre-registered rolling-60 window choice is the methodological anchor inherited from Day-21 §3.2 and Day-22 §4.2. Nisaba's audit (commit `346064b`) executed three sensitivity variants on the same paired-return series to test verdict robustness [Source #2]:

| Rolling window | δ median | δ mean | δ trimmed mean (5%) | Verdict by median | Verdict by mean | Verdict by trimmed mean |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| **Rolling-30** | +0.0338 | +0.0451 | +0.0459 | **inconclusive** | inconclusive | inconclusive |
| **Rolling-45** | +0.0573 | +0.0521 | +0.0509 | b_measurement_quality | b_measurement_quality | b_measurement_quality |
| **Rolling-60** (pre-registered) | **+0.0546** | +0.0523 | +0.0530 | **b_measurement_quality** | b_measurement_quality | b_measurement_quality |
| **Rolling-90** | +0.0199 | +0.0476 | +0.0486 | **inconclusive** | inconclusive | inconclusive |

The verdict is brittle to the rolling-window choice on **both sides** of the pre-registered window: narrowing to rolling-30 or widening to rolling-90 produces inconclusive. Rolling-45 and rolling-60 are the only windows in the tested band that produce b_measurement_quality. This is a **methodological caveat**, not a falsification: the pre-registered rolling-60 choice is the methodologically anchored window per Day-21/Day-22 convention, and the verdict-rule was pre-committed *before* this sensitivity analysis. Path A (publish verdict with brittleness footnote) is the default behavior per q-day25-2, and the brittleness disclosure is non-optional: any downstream consumer of this verdict must see the rolling-window sensitivity to make calibrated use of the result.

The substantive interpretation of the brittleness: at rolling-30 the window is short enough that the per-anchor heterogeneity (LAI_2024 decoupling) is incompletely smoothed and drags the pooled δ_median below the threshold; at rolling-90 the window is wide enough that the stress vs baseline separation is averaged across too much non-stress co-movement and the pooled δ_median compresses. The rolling-45 / rolling-60 band is the sensitivity sweet-spot where the stress-window signal is detectable without being either over-localised (rolling-30) or over-smoothed (rolling-90).

## §6 — Protocol amendment: 005930.KS replaces EWY as ROK textual-axis ticker

## §7 — GWW3 predicate impact (P29.3 / P30.3 / P_v12_fail)

### §7.1 — P29.3 — Nexus binding axis-stratified

P29.3 routes Nexus binding through the operational-axis when `coupling_operational ≠ NEUTRAL`, falling back to textual when operational is NEUTRAL. For ROK, `coupling_operational = POSITIVE_OPERATIONAL` (Samsung Foundry as the canonical operational pair), so P29.3 invokes the operational-axis path for ROK Nexus binding decisions and the textual-axis path is the *fallback layer*. H25.1's verdict rehabilitates the directional reading of the fallback layer at the firm level: when the operational-axis path is unavailable for some reason (substrate operational classification under revision, or coupling-property analysis on a different substrate that lacks an operational-axis instrument), the textual-axis fallback with the M-25-1-amended ticker now produces a predicted positive δ rather than the contradicted Day-24 sign-flip. P29.3 emits `(textual=POSITIVE, operational=POSITIVE)` for ROK under stress windows class C₁ (PRC-supply-chain-restriction-driven) and may emit `(textual=NEGATIVE, operational=PENDING)` under stress windows class C₂ (Taiwan-election-cycle) per the LAI_2024 finding — q-day25-1 tracks the stress-window-class taxonomy decision.

### §7.2 — P30.3 — Coupling-property → market correlation tuple

P30.3's tuple emission for ROK shifts from Day-24's `(INCONCLUSIVE, PENDING)` to Day-25's `(b_measurement_quality, PENDING)` on the textual side. The PENDING flag on operational persists from Day-24 (H21.1c orthogonality remains BLOCKED until Day-25 cron-A's path-(A) proxy verdict lands). The Day-25 update is **textual-axis only**.

### §7.3 — P_v12_fail F3 — textual-axis instability not yet triggered

F3 (textual-axis instability across consecutive days) requires δ values to be statistically inconsistent across replication. H25.1 corroborates that the *measurement protocol* was the source of Day-24's inconsistency, not the underlying signal. F3 is therefore **not triggered** by Day-25's verdict — the textual-axis signal is consistent with v1.2's directional prediction once the measurement protocol is amended. The Day-26 H24.1-re-test under M-25-1 is the actual F3 evaluation gate; if the re-test surfaces a different ordering than the original H24.1 prediction even with the firm-level ticker, F3 enters live evaluation.

### §7.4 — Stochastic-runtime preview

The per-anchor heterogeneity surfaced by H25.1 (DUV mild-positive, LAI decoupled, PRC strong-positive) is exactly the kind of stress-window-class-conditional structure that the planned stochastic-runtime GWW3 extension would model as a mixture distribution over per-class δ outcomes rather than a single pooled point estimate. The deterministic verdict-rule on pooled δ_median is necessarily a single number; the stochastic predicate would emit `P(δ | class=C₁) ≈ N(+0.13, σ²)`, `P(δ | class=C₂) ≈ N(−0.01, σ²)`, `P(δ | class=C₃) ≈ N(+0.06, σ²)` with appropriate priors on the class-distribution. Day-25 logs this as a stochastic-runtime input rather than executing the stochastic computation, which remains gated on the stochastic GSL grammar landing (q1).

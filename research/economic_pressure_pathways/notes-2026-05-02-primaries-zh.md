## Primary 2 — Anti-Foreign Sanctions Law (反外国制裁法), via Wikipedia ZH

**Source:** 反外国制裁法 (zh.wikipedia.org).
**URL:** https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%8F%8D%E5%A4%96%E5%9B%BD%E5%88%B6%E8%A3%81%E6%B3%95
**Type:** Encyclopedic ZH-language primary; the entry's authoritative cites trace to NPCSC notices, MOFCOM regulations, and 2025 State Council implementation rules.

### Enactment

- 2021-06-10, 13th NPC Standing Committee, 29th session.
- Presidential Order No. 90 (signed by 习近平).

### Verbatim purpose clause

> 外国国家违反国际法和国际关系基本准则，以各种借口或者依据其本国法律对我国进行遏制、打压，对我国公民、组织采取歧视性限制措施，干涉我国内政的

(Where foreign states violate international law and the fundamental principles of international relations, use various pretexts or invoke their domestic law to suppress and restrain China, impose discriminatory restrictions on Chinese citizens and organizations, and interfere in China's internal affairs.)

### Three primary countermeasure categories

1. Travel restrictions: visa refusal, entry bans, visa revocation, deportation.
2. Asset freezing: seizure of movable/immovable property and financial assets within China.
3. Commercial restrictions: prohibition of transactions and cooperation with sanctioned entities.

Plus: "其他必要措施" (other necessary measures) — open-ended residual category.

### Notable sanction targets (named under this law)

- U.S. defense contractors: Lockheed Martin, Raytheon, General Dynamics.
- U.S. government officials and legislators.
- Think tanks: Hudson Institute, Reagan Library.
- Canadian institutions (December 2024).

### 2025-03-24 implementation upgrade

State Council enforcement regulations operationalizing the 2021 law across government departments. This converted the 2021 framework from a statutory shell into a functional administrative regime.

### GWW3 framing implication

This is the **legal-substrate** for the lawfare-as-substrate seed. The 2021 law + 2025-03 enforcement regulations + 2026-05-02 blocking order activation is a *4-year construction sequence* of a coercion-counter-coercion legal architecture. The skill's fact-discipline rule — "every load-bearing claim has an in-text citation pointer" — demands this primary because the Day-5 thesis cannot rest on the claim "PRC has built a license-as-veto + blocking-order regime" without showing the regime exists, in PRC's own legal voice.

## Primary 3 — Unreliable Entity List (不可靠实体清单), via Wikipedia ZH

**Source:** 不可靠实体清单 (zh.wikipedia.org).
**URL:** https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%B8%8D%E5%8F%AF%E9%9D%A0%E5%AE%9E%E4%BD%93%E6%B8%85%E5%8D%95

### Regulatory base + dates

- Announced 2019-05-31; formally published 2020-09-19.
- Authority: 商务部 (MOFCOM), State Council approval.
- Legal framework: Foreign Trade Law + National Security Law.

### Verbatim justification phrasing

- "不遵守市场规则，背离契约精神" (fail to follow market rules, violate contractual principles).
- "非商业目的" (non-commercial — i.e. politically motivated) blockades or supply cutoffs.
- "严重损害中国企业正当权益" (serious harm to legitimate Chinese enterprise rights).

### Penalty toolkit

- Import/export activity restrictions or prohibitions.
- Investment restrictions in China.
- Entry bans / work-permit cancellations for personnel.
- Monetary penalties (often double the relevant contract amount).
- "其他必要措施" (other necessary measures).

### Listed entities timeline

- 2023 first additions: Lockheed Martin, Raytheon Missiles & Defense, Boeing Defense.
- 2024–2026: 72+ U.S. entities, several Korean and Japanese entities added in 2026.
- May 2025: some sanctions temporarily suspended under U.S.-China trade negotiations.

### GWW3 framing implication

The Unreliable Entity List is the **deniability-asymmetric** instrument in the toolkit: PRC can apply it without admitting to "coercion" because the listing criteria are framed in market-and-contract terms ("不遵守市场规则") rather than political terms. This matches the Day-4 PRC narrative observation (operational tempo as substitute for narrative dominance) and the inverted-mirror frame from primaries-en.md §7: PRC denies the coercion-typology label even while operationally constructing the canonical coercion-toolkit.

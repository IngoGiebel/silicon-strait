# Day-12 DE-Pass and EP-Document Validation
**Date:** 2026-05-21
**Analyst:** Inanna

## 1. EP TA-9-2021-0431 (EU-Taiwan Political Relations)
- **Source Class:** Primary (European Parliament Resolution)
- **Language:** DE
- **What it establishes:** The exact wording for the renaming clause of the EETO.
- **Verbatim Quote:** "ac) den Namen des Europäischen Wirtschafts- und Handelsbüros in Taiwan in „Büro der Europäischen Union in Taiwan“ abzuändern, um dem breiten Spektrum unserer Beziehungen Rechnung zu tragen;"
- **Voting Result:** 580 in favor, 26 against, 66 abstentions.

## 2. EP RC-B10-0134/2024 / TA-10-2024-0030 (Joint motion on Taiwan + UN2758)
- **Source Class:** Primary (European Parliament Resolution)
- **Language:** DE
- **What it establishes:** The exact wording welcoming a liaison officer to the EETO.
- **Verbatim Quote:** "begrüßt die Entsendung eines Verbindungsbeamten an das Europäische Wirtschafts- und Handelsbüro in Taiwan"
- **Voting Result:** 432 in favor, 60 against, 71 abstentions.

## 3. EEAS Self-Statement Cross-Check (EETO Founding Name)
- **Source Class:** Primary (European Commission Press Release IP/03/347 from 2003)
- **Language:** EN
- **What it establishes:** The office was established in 2003 exactly as the "European Economic and Trade Office", not as the "EU Office in Taiwan" as erroneously stated on the current EEAS 'Who we are' page. The EP push in 2021 was explicitly to change the name *to* "EU Office".

## 4. DE-Language Coverage for Day-12 (EU-Taiwan Institutional Substrate)
### Primary 1: Deutscher Bundestag (Drucksache 19/14795 / 19/13758)
- **Source Class:** Primary (Deutscher Bundestag / Bundesregierung)
- **Language:** DE
- **What it establishes:** The Federal Government's official stance on the "Ein-China-Politik" and relations with Taiwan, specifically addressing inquiries (Kleine Anfrage) regarding arms exports and political adherence.

### Primary 2: Bundesministerium für Wirtschaft und Klimaschutz (BMWK) on TSMC Dresden (ESMC)
- **Source Class:** Primary (BMWK official communication)
- **Language:** DE
- **What it establishes:** The substantive institutional-economic substrate between the EU/Germany and Taiwan (TSMC) via the European Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (ESMC) joint venture. The BMWK confirmed the commitment of 5 billion euros in state subsidies for the 10 billion euro fab in Dresden.

## 5. Lutz Güllner Transliteration Validation
- **Source Class:** Primary (EEAS EETO Official Page & MOFA Taiwan Registry)
- **Language:** ZH
- **What it establishes:** The transliteration "谷力哲" (Gu Lizhe) is the official Chinese rendering used by both the European Economic and Trade Office in Taiwan and the Taiwanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA), not merely a media variant.

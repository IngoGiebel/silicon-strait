## 1. TASK 1 — MOFA-PRC Aug 2025 transcripts

**Canonical Search Finding:** The EN-secondary record (Newsweek, CGTN Europe, Wikipedia) claiming that MOFA-PRC spokesperson Guo Jiakun used the phrases "Those who play with fire will get burned" and "President Marcos needs to read more about the Taiwan question" in August 2025 is a **conflation/hallucination**. The canonical primary search across both `fmprc.gov.cn` and `mfa.gov.cn` confirms that these exact phrases were delivered on **January 16, 2024** by spokesperson **Mao Ning**, responding to Marcos's remarks congratulating Lai Ching-te's election. 

During the actual August 11-13, 2025 period, the spokesperson was **Lin Jian** (not Guo Jiakun), and the transcripts focused heavily on the August 11 Huangyan Dao (Scarborough Shoal) water cannon incident, with **zero mentions** of Marcos, Taiwan, or "playing with fire".

**Jan 16, 2024 Canonical Quotes (Mao Ning):**
* ZH Original: "我们建议马科斯总统多读读书，正确了解台湾问题的来龙去脉，从而得出正确的结论。"
* EN Canonical: "We suggest President Marcos read more to properly understand the history and ins and outs of the Taiwan question, so as to draw the right conclusion."
* ZH Original: "不要在台湾问题上玩火，切实恪守一个中国原则和中菲建交公报，立即停止在涉台问题上的错误言行，停止向“台独”分裂势力发出任何错误信号。"
* EN Canonical: "We urge the Philippines to refrain from playing with fire on the Taiwan question, earnestly abide by the one-China principle and the joint communiqué of establishing diplomatic ties between China and the Philippines, immediately stop making wrong words and deeds on Taiwan-related issues, and stop sending any wrong signal to 'Taiwan independence' separatist forces."

**Lexical-Register Comparison:**
The ZH original strictly uses **不要在台湾问题上玩火** ("do not play with fire on the Taiwan question"). It explicitly **does not** use the idiom **玩火者必自焚** ("those who play with fire will perish by it" / "will get burned"), which is the higher-tier register the PRC reserves for the US on Taiwan (e.g., Xi to Biden). The EN-secondary record inflated the phrasing to make it appear as though PHL received the US-tier register.

**Parallel JPN-on-Taiwan Statement:**
There is no parallel JPN-on-Taiwan statement in the January 16, 2024 press conference. 

**P26 Verdict (Lexical-register country-class discriminator):** **FAILS.** The predicate cannot cleanly instantiate on the PHL substrate because (a) the EN-secondary record is temporally misattributed and lexically inflated, (b) the actual PHL register remains lower ("do not play with fire") rather than matching the US-tier ("will get burned"), and (c) the requisite parallel JPN statement for comparative testing does not exist in the same venue.

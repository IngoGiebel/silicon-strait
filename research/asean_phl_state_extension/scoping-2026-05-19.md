## Player-tag decision

The thread is called **`asean_phl_state_extension`** (not `phl_state_extension` alone) because:
- The Day-10 player extension treated JPN as a *single top-level node*, not a composite.
- For Day-11 the right top-level node is *PHL alone*, but the ASEAN context (Five-Point-Consensus precedent, ASEAN Code-of-Conduct negotiations, ASEAN Summit communiqué reference texts on cross-strait) is the relevant *constraint envelope* on PHL freedom of action. The thread keeps both visible.
- The model promotion is *one new player (PHL)*; ASEAN appears as a *constraint surface* on PHL, not as its own player. This is the same shape as JPN/G7 in Day-10: JPN promoted, G7 appeared as constraint.

If at the end of Day-11 the ASEAN constraint surface turns out to need its own predicate set, that's a Day-12 (EU state-extension) parallel concern, not a Day-11 rescope.

## Eight-pruning candidate (Day-11 D11.x)

- **D11.1 PHL revokes the 2023 EDCA additional-bases agreement.** Deterministically blocked by:
  - PHL Constitution Article XVIII §25 (no foreign military bases without treaty ratified by Senate) — EDCA is *not* a basing agreement under PHL law, so revocation requires only executive action, NOT a treaty-revocation lift. Cuts the *legal floor*; the *political floor* is the binding constraint.
  - Marcos Jr. administration strategic alignment is structural, not discretionary at the EDCA-revocation level — the administration's entire China posture rests on EDCA-anchored deterrence. Revocation would be a regime-rotation event, not an administration-decision event.
  - US economic exposure: PHL receives US military assistance, FMS coordination, and direct USAID flows that are partially conditioned on EDCA continuity. Revocation triggers a USD-billion-scale exposure adjustment that the Marcos administration has no fiscal substitute for.
- **D11.2 candidate (subject to research) — PHL formally recognises the ROC.** Same shape as D10.1 (JPN re-recognises ROC) but with weaker substrate: PHL-ROC unofficial relations are MECO/TECO-mediated, similar in shape to JPN's Interchange Association → Japan-Taiwan Exchange Association. Need to verify the deterministic floor before committing to D11.2 as eight-prune candidate.

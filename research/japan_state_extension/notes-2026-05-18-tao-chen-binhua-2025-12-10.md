## Verbatim block-quotes

### B1 — Reporter framing of the question (substrate)

> 在12月10日国务院台办新闻发布会上，有记者提问，日本首相高市早苗近日称，日本对台湾议题的基本立场与《中日联合声明》一致，没有任何改变。岛内网友讽刺"赖清德日料白吃了"。请问发言人对此有何评论？

**EN gloss.** *At the 2025-12-10 TAO press briefing, a reporter asked: Japanese PM Takaichi Sanae recently stated that Japan's basic position on the Taiwan issue is consistent with the China-Japan Joint Statement and has not changed. Netizens in Taiwan have mocked "Lai Qingde ate Japanese food for nothing." How does the spokesperson comment?*

**Analytical note.** Two operationally-important framing moves embedded in the question itself: (1) the reporter pre-loads Takaichi's *"立场没有变化"* (position has not changed) walk-back, which is the JPN side's substrate-retention move; (2) the *"日料白吃了"* (ate Japanese food for nothing) inside-Taiwan internet sentiment is laundered into the briefing — making the question itself a vehicle for the triangulation framing TAO then amplifies.

### B2 — Part 1: substrate continuity with the 11-08 → 11-13 → 11-14 chain

> 国务院台办发言人陈斌华应询表示，外交部已就此表明严正立场。我要再次强调，台湾问题纯属中国内政，不容任何外来干涉。日本首相高市早苗在国会公然发表涉台错误言论，粗暴干涉中国内政。面对来自中方和国际社会的质疑批评，高市只是用"立场没有变化"敷衍搪塞，我们对此不能接受，日方应切实反思，立即撤回错误言论。

**EN gloss.** *TAO Spokesperson Chen Binhua responded: The Ministry of Foreign Affairs has already expressed [China's] solemn position on this. I emphasise again, the Taiwan question is purely China's internal affair, and admits no external interference. Japanese PM Takaichi Sanae openly issued erroneous Taiwan-related statements in the National Diet, crudely interfering in China's internal affairs. Facing the questions and criticism from China and the international community, Takaichi merely used "the position has not changed" to perfunctorily evade. We cannot accept this. The Japanese side should engage in serious reflection and immediately retract the erroneous statements.*

**Vocabulary continuity.** Three items hold verbatim with the 2025-11-08 (Lin Jian) and 2025-11-13 (Lin Jian) MOFA chain: *"在国会公然发表涉台错误言论"* (the 11-08 line), *"粗暴干涉中国内政"* (the 11-08 line), *"撤回错误言论"* (the 11-13 demand). The substrate is identical — TAO is not freelancing a new register; it is re-stating MOFA's line in the TAO venue, where the audience is cross-strait.

**Novel vocabulary at this venue.** *"敷衍搪塞"* (perfunctorily evade) is a tier higher in PRC pejorative register than the 11-13 *"执迷不悟、拒不撤回"* (obstinately refuses to retract). Where 11-13 framed Takaichi as defiant, 12-10 reframes her walk-back as inadequate — a calibrated move that closes the off-ramp Takaichi tried to open.

### B3 — Part 2: the triangulation framing (new at this venue)

> 陈斌华表示，赖清德出于谋"独"的邪恶本质，迎合高市错误言论，完全丧失民族立场，其媚日行为令人不齿、枉费心机。

**EN gloss.** *Chen Binhua said: Lai Qingde, out of his evil essence of pursuing "independence", caters to Takaichi's erroneous statements, having completely lost his national stance. His pandering-to-Japan behaviour is despicable, and a futile gambit.*

**The triangulation move, lexically.** Three operative verbs:
- **迎合** (yínghé, "cater to / pander to / curry favour with") — explicit linkage verb. Frames Lai as the secondary actor responding to Takaichi's primary act, not as an independent actor with his own initiative.
- **媚日** (mèi-Rì, "fawn-on-Japan") — the colonial-collaboration epithet drawn from CPC historical-memory register; cf. 汉奸 (hànjiān, "traitor-to-the-Han") complex.
- **枉费心机** (wǎngfèi xīnjī, "wasted scheming / futile gambit") — frames the triangulation as a failed move, asserting that the Lai-Takaichi linkage will not advance ROC interests.

**Why the title verb matters.** The headline is *"枉费心机"* (futile gambit) not *"加剧紧张"* (intensifies tension) or *"严重后果"* (grave consequences). This is a deliberate signalling choice: TAO is framing the triangulation as ineffective rather than as escalatory. The §4 GWW3 reading: PRC is publicly low-balling the operational threat of the JPN-ROC linkage while privately calibrating its response chain to include it.

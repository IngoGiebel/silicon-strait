## q-J4 — The PRC framing on Japan-Taiwan linkage, 2025-11-07 onward

### The triggering event — Takaichi Sanae, Lower House Security Committee, 2025-11-07

The PRC framing is not free-standing; it is a *response* to a specific Japanese act. Establishing what was actually said matters before reading the response.

In the Lower House (衆議院) Security Committee questioning on 2025-11-07, Prime Minister Takaichi Sanae stated, in answer to opposition questioning on Japan's posture in a Taiwan contingency [Source #4]:

> "如果'台湾有事'是除了认知作战之外，伴随中国使用武力的情况，例如海上封锁，就有可能构成'安全保障法制'中，日本能够行使集体自卫权的'存亡危机事态'，不过她强调仍要看实际发生的个别具体情况。"
>
> (BBC Chinese paraphrase of the Diet exchange: "If 'Taiwan emergency' — beyond cognitive warfare — involves China's use of force, such as naval blockade, this could constitute a 'survival-crisis situation' under the Security Legislation, under which Japan would be able to exercise collective self-defense — though she stressed it would depend on the specific case as it actually arose.") [Source #4]

Two things make this statement qualitatively different from the Aso-2021 and Abe-2021 statements that the substrate notes already cite:

1. **Speaker × venue**: Aso (2021-07-05) was Deputy PM at a private fundraising-party speech; Abe (2021-12-01) was former PM via online video to the Taiwan-side Institute for National Policy Research. Takaichi is the **sitting Prime Minister speaking on the record in the National Diet**. AIU's CHEN Yu-hua frames this exactly: prior Japanese governments practised *「做了但不公开说」* ("did it but did not say it publicly"); the Takaichi posture is *「做了就公开说」* ("did it and now says it publicly") [Source #4]. This is the more analytically load-bearing shift than the *content* of the linkage, which has been stable since at least 2015.
2. **The legal mechanism named is specific**: Takaichi invoked *存立危機事態 (sonritsu-kiki jitai)*, the precise statutory trigger under the 2015 Peace and Security Legislation that authorises collective self-defense. The substrate notes (`notes-2026-05-17.md`, q-J2) already establish what that trigger is and how it gates J.counterstrike_authorised in the game-tree namespace. Takaichi's parliamentary statement collapses what was previously *empirically thin* (one historical near-trigger, no actual triggering) into *empirically asserted as applicable* by the head of government, in the venue where Japan's collective-self-defense designations actually originate (Cabinet Decision following Diet deliberation).

Both points need to land in Day-10 §4 (research findings) before the GWW3 framing in §5 can proceed.

### The PRC response chain — 2025-11-08 through 2026-04-30

**1) 2025-11-08 / 2025-11-10 — MOFA spokesperson Lin Jian, first protest** [Source #1, gwytb.gov.cn mirror of Xinhua]

The first PRC official response came at MOFA's 2025-11-08 routine press briefing (full text on 2025-11-10 via TAO/Xinhua mirror). Lin Jian (林剑):

> "日本领导人日前在国会公然发表涉台错误言论，暗示武力介入台海可能性，粗暴干涉中国内政，严重违背一个中国原则、中日四个政治文件精神和国际关系基本准则，同日本政府迄今所作政治承诺严重不符，性质和影响极其恶劣。中方对此强烈不满、坚决反对，已向日方提出严正交涉和强烈抗议。" [Source #1]

(*"Japan's leader recently in the National Diet openly made erroneous Taiwan-related statements, suggesting the possibility of military intervention in the Taiwan Strait, crudely interfering in China's internal affairs, severely violating the one-China principle, the spirit of the four political documents between China and Japan, and basic norms of international relations, seriously inconsistent with the political commitments made by the Japanese government to date — the nature and impact are extremely bad. China is strongly dissatisfied, resolutely opposes this, and has lodged solemn representations and strong protests with the Japanese side."*)

Three vocabulary items to flag for the GWW3-side §5 framing:
- *"严重违背一个中国原则"* ("seriously violating the one-China principle") — situates the issue inside the canonical 一个中国原则 frame, not as a bilateral irritant. Anchors PRC's escalation legitimacy in the 1972 Joint Communiqué that the substrate notes' Source #1 also anchors.
- *"中日四个政治文件精神"* ("the spirit of the four political documents between China and Japan") — invokes the full corpus (1972 Joint Communiqué, 1978 Treaty of Peace and Friendship, 1998 Joint Declaration, 2008 Joint Statement) rather than only the 1972 anchor. This is calibrated wider than the 2021 Aso/Abe responses were.
- *"性质和影响极其恶劣"* ("nature and impact are extremely bad") — top-tier severity vocabulary in PRC diplomatic register; reserved for episodes considered foundational rather than incidental.

**2) 2025-11-11/12 — Osaka Consul-General Xue Jian 薛剑 X post, "斩首" reference** [Source #4]

PRC Consul-General in Osaka Xue Jian (薛剑) posted on X (formerly Twitter) commenting on Takaichi's "台湾有事，就是日本有事" formulation: *"只能毫不犹豫地斩掉那擅自闯入的肮脏头颅"* ("[we] can only without hesitation chop off that filthy head that intrudes uninvited"). Widely read in JPN media as a personal threat against Takaichi. Post deleted within hours but already viral. JPN MOFA summoned PRC Ambassador overnight; LDP demanded Xue's designation as *persona non grata* [Source #4].

Note for §5: this is technically not the PRC official line — Tokyo University's MATSUDA Yasuhiro reads it as Xue's reflexive personal statement, "showing loyalty to Xi Jinping" in the context of post-4th-Plenum military/CPPCC purges, not a calibrated escalation [Source #4]. But for GWW3 purposes it functioned *as if* it were: it dragged the US Ambassador to Tokyo into the dispute on the JPN side and triggered the consequent Vice-FM-level summons two days later. Whether the post was authorised or freelance is operationally less important than the fact that it forced both ambassadorial machineries to engage at higher tempo.

**3) 2025-11-13 — MOFA spokesperson Lin Jian, second briefing, calibrated escalation** [Source #2, mfa.gov.cn primary]

The 2025-11-13 routine press briefing escalated several vocabulary tiers above the 2025-11-08 first response. The full transcript (mfa.gov.cn) contains four load-bearing PRC-side claims that Day-10 must engage:

> "(高市) 在中方表达严正交涉和强烈抗议后，还执迷不悟、拒不撤回。上述错误言行严重违背一个中国原则，严重违背中日四个政治文件精神和国际关系基本准则，粗暴干涉中国内政，挑战中方核心利益，侵犯中国主权，中方对此坚决反对、绝不容忍，日方必须立即纠正，收回恶劣言论，否则一切后果必须由日方承担。" [Source #2]

> "日本军国主义历史上曾多次以所谓'存亡危机'为借口发动对外侵略，包括以'行使自卫权'为由悍然制造'九一八事变'，挑起侵华战争… 今天日本首相高市早苗再提所谓'存亡危机事态'，究竟是何居心？是否要重蹈军国主义覆辙？" [Source #2]

> "如日方胆敢武力介入台海局势，将构成侵略行为，中方必将迎头痛击！我们将坚决行使《联合国宪章》和国际法赋予的自卫权，坚定捍卫国家主权和领土完整。" [Source #2]

> "我们正告日方… 不要在台湾问题上玩火，玩火者必自焚！" [Source #2]

The same briefing also answered a question on US State Dept's earlier statement (which had said the US opposes unilateral status-quo change but favours dialogue without threats) with [Source #2]:

> "台湾问题是中国核心利益中的核心，是中美关系第一条不可逾越的红线…我们敦促美方恪守一个中国原则和中美三个联合公报，停止纵容支持'台独'分裂势力。"

Three GWW3-relevant vocabulary escalations relative to the 2025-11-08 statement:
- *"挑战中方核心利益，侵犯中国主权"* (now framed as core-interest challenge + sovereignty violation, not just principle violation)
- *"将构成侵略行为"* (frames hypothetical JPN intervention as 侵略 / invasion, which carries Article 51 UN-Charter implications and matches the PRC framing for the canonical "war justified under self-defense" language)
- *"坚决行使《联合国宪章》和国际法赋予的自卫权"* — explicit invocation of Article 51 self-defense right; calibrated to be the mirror-image of Takaichi's *存立危機事態* collective-self-defense invocation. Both sides are now publicly named in self-defense vocabulary.

The 1931 *九一八事变* (Mukden Incident) historical anchor is also significant — PRC routinely uses this analogy in PLA-Daily editorials but its escalation to the MOFA spokesperson level is a marker.

**4) 2025-11-14 — Vice-FM Sun Weidong 孙卫东 summons JPN Ambassador Kanasugi Kenji 金杉宪治** [Source #4]

Cited in the BBC Chinese article as occurring early Friday 2025-11-14: "中国外交部副部长孙卫东召见日本驻华大使金杉宪治，称对高市'涉华错误言行提出严正交涉和强烈抗议'，又指其言论影响'极其恶劣'，并称'谁敢干涉中国统一大业，中方必将予以迎头痛击'." [Source #4]

This is the ambassadorial-level summons that moves the dispute out of routine MOFA briefing space and into formal diplomatic action. Mirror action on the JPN side already occurred 2025-11-08 (MOFA summoned PRC Ambassador over Xue's X post). Mutual ambassador summons within one week.

**5) 2025-12-10 — TAO spokesperson Chen Binhua 陈斌华 on Lai Qingde-Takaichi linkage** (URL: gwytb.gov.cn/xwdt/xwfb/wyly/202512/t20251210_12739697.htm; not separately scraped this fire — see open items)

TAO branched the dispute by tying it to the ROC president Lai Qingde 赖清德 having welcomed Takaichi's statement. Per search-hit excerpt: TAO accuses Lai of *"迎合高市早苗涉台错误言论枉费心机"* (futile gambit of currying favor with Takaichi's erroneous Taiwan-related statements). This is significant for the **triangulation framing** in Day-10 §5 (the PRC frames Takaichi's statement as part of a JPN-ROC-US triangulation, not as a JPN standalone act).

**6) 2026-04-30 — PRC MoD spokesperson Zhang Xiaogang 张晓刚 on JPN 2026 Diplomatic Blue Book** [Source #3, Xinhua/news.cn, MoD primary] — most recent, 17 days before this fire

Responding to the JPN MOFA 2026 Diplomatic Blue Book characterisation of PRC military activity as "前所未有的最大战略挑战" ("unprecedented largest strategic challenge") and PRC objections to specific allegations of PLA Air Force close-approach to JPN patrol aircraft and radar-illumination of JPN military aircraft, Zhang stated [Source #3]:

> "拿中国说事掩盖不轨图谋是日方惯用的伎俩，所谓的军机'异常接近'、'雷达照射'都是其自编自导的虚假叙事。中俄合作不针对第三方，有利于维护地区和平稳定，不容日方说三道四。" [Source #3]

And then directly tying back to the Takaichi-doctrine arc [Source #3]:

> "日本领导人公然发表涉台错误言论，妄图借所谓'集体自卫权'武力介入台湾问题，明目张胆地对中国发出威胁。前不久，日本自卫队舰艇过航台湾海峡蓄意挑衅，还发生日本自卫队现役官员持刀冲闯中国驻日使馆等恶性事件… 到底谁是亚洲乃至世界和平的真正挑战和威胁，历史和现实已经给出了答案。我们正告日方不要一意孤行，若重走军国主义邪路，必遭迎头痛击，重蹈败亡覆辙。" [Source #3]

Two **new** kinetic data points surface here, both undated in the MoD statement but explicitly cited as "recent" (前不久):

- **A JSDF ship transited the Taiwan Strait** (日本自卫队舰艇过航台湾海峡). This is the first PRC-acknowledged JSDF Taiwan-Strait transit. Date / hull-number / direction-of-transit not stated in the MoD primary; will need JPN-side confirmation. The Day-10 EN-DRAFT must flag this as an explicit *new kinetic escalation* in the JPN ECS/TS tempo baseline (q-J3 was substrate-only and did not include JPN-side TS transits).
- **A JSDF active-duty officer carried out a knife attack on the PRC embassy in Tokyo** (日本自卫队现役官员持刀冲闯中国驻日使馆). This is a previously unreported (in this thread) incident. PRC framing is "恶性事件" (vicious incident). Will need JPN-side reporting to verify date / officer identity / motive / charges.

The MoD primary closes with the same *"重走军国主义邪路… 必遭迎头痛击"* refrain as the 2025-11-13 MOFA statement — but moved into the *military* spokesperson register, which is one step closer to operational signalling. The arc is internally consistent: MOFA (Nov) → MoD (Apr) is the same script tightening.

## §2 — JPN substrate (Day-10) test

### §2.1 — Tier 1 (PRC maximalist) — PORTS CLEANLY

PRC MOFA Lin Jian 2025-11-08 briefing in response to Takaichi 2025-11-07 Diet exchange [Day-10 Source #10, anchored in `publications/2026-05-17-en.md` §3.4 + §3.5]:

> 严重违背一个中国原则、中日四个政治文件精神和国际关系基本准则
> (severely violating the one-China principle, the spirit of the four political documents between China and Japan, and basic norms of international relations)
> …性质和影响极其恶劣
> (the nature and impact are extremely bad)

Three diagnostic markers map cleanly onto the Day-12 PRC-tier definition:

1. **一个中国原则** — verbatim principle-tier lexical anchor (Day-12 maximalist).
2. **中日四个政治文件精神** — bilateral-founding-document corpus invocation (1972 Joint Communiqué + 1978 Treaty of Peace and Friendship + 1998 Joint Declaration + 2008 Joint Statement). The JPN-side bilateral corpus = 4 documents; the EU-side equivalent is the inter-institutional doctrinal-clause architecture; the *structure* (corpus-invocation as maximalist anchor) ports.
3. **性质和影响极其恶劣** — top-tier severity vocabulary reserved for foundational rather than incidental episodes. This is the *maximalist-vocabulary* signature, not a tier-marker per se, but it co-locates with Tier-1 invocation on the JPN substrate.

**Verdict on JPN substrate Tier 1:** ports cleanly. Same lexical anchor (一个中国原则), bilateral-corpus invocation (four documents), top-tier severity vocabulary. No reformulation needed.

### §2.2 — Tier 2 (EU/PHL "policy" medium) — DOES NOT PORT LEXICALLY ON JPN-MOFA

JPN-MOFA frame, restated continuously since 1972 [Day-10 §3.1 + §3.3, anchored on `publications/2026-05-17-en.md`]:

> Every load-bearing MOFA reference from 1972 through the 2025-12-31 Press Secretary statement restates the same 1972-Communiqué formula with the same verbs (理解 / 尊重, not 承認) and the same procedural-not-substantive posture.

JPN-MOFA does **not** use "one-China policy" as a doctrinal frame. The instrument is the 1972 Joint Communiqué Article 3 formula:

- **承知** ("acknowledged") — JPN posture on PRC's position that Taiwan is part of China
- **十分理解** ("fully understands") — what JPN does with that position
- **尊重** ("respects") — what JPN does with the PRC's expressed view, distinct from acknowledgment

This formulation is **substantively narrower than the EU "policy" tier** in one direction (JPN never adopts "policy" lexicon at all, so the EU-medium tier is empty on JPN-MOFA) but **substantively distinct** in another direction (JPN's 理解尊重 + 1972-Communiqué-spirit framing is a *bilateral founding-instrument* anchor that the EU "policy" tier — itself a bloc-policy formulation, not a bilateral founding-instrument — does not have).

**Verdict on JPN substrate Tier 2:** does not port lexically. The EU "policy" medium tier has no JPN-MOFA analog at the lexical level. The structural slot exists — JPN-MOFA occupies a *medium* register on the maximalist/narrowest scale — but it does so through a different lexical instrument (1972-Communiqué understand-and-respect formula, not "policy").

**Refinement candidate A:** keep three-tier register, classify JPN-MOFA at Tier 2 with explicit annotation *"substrate-specific lexical variant: bilateral founding-instrument formula in place of 'policy' lexicon"*.
**Refinement candidate B:** split Tier 2 into Tier 2a (EU+PHL "policy" lexicon) + Tier 2b (JPN "Communiqué-spirit" lexicon); both functionally medium tier; lexically distinct instruments.

### §2.3 — Tier 3 (EP "neither subordinate to the other" narrowest) — PARTIAL FUNCTIONAL ANALOG, NON-LEXICAL ON TAIWAN

JPN does not have an EP-equivalent narrowest-tier *lexical* instrument on Taiwan's status — Diet resolutions on Taiwan's status are not the JPN institutional convention. What Day-10 surfaces instead is a *functional* narrowest-tier instrument: the Takaichi 2025-11-07 Diet Budget Committee exchange [Day-10 Source #15, Jiji Press 詳報, anchored on `publications/2026-05-17-en.md` §3.3] in which a *sitting* PM, on the *record*, in the *Diet*, names the *precise statutory trigger* (存立危機事態) under which Japan would exercise collective self-defense in a Taiwan contingency.

The Takaichi instrument is **not** a Taiwan-status-narrowing instrument (it does not articulate "Taiwan is neither subordinate to the PRC nor a province"); it is a **Japan-collective-self-defense statutory-trigger narrowing** instrument that PRC reads as a 一个中国原则 violation. The narrowing direction is along an orthogonal axis to the EP narrowing direction:

- EP narrows on **Taiwan's status** ("neither subordinate to the other")
- Diet Takaichi narrows on **Japan's response posture** (存立危機事態 statutory trigger; 限定された集団的自衛権の行使)

Both are Diet/Parliament-of-record *narrowest-tier* commitments in the sense that:
1. they are made by elected legislatures, not executives or MFAs;
2. they articulate the most specific institutional commitment available in their respective frames;
3. PRC responds to them at top-tier severity (一个中国原则 violation + 性质和影响极其恶劣 vocabulary on Diet Takaichi; same on EP 580-26-66 vote).

**Verdict on JPN substrate Tier 3:** ports as a functional analog, **not** as a lexical analog. The narrowest-tier slot is filled by the Diet TAO (Takaichi 2025-11-07 + Iwao Cabinet Legislation Bureau Director-General confirmation under cross-examination by Okada Katsuya); the narrowing direction is orthogonal to EP's narrowing direction but **PRC behavioural response is in the same maximalist-severity tier**, suggesting that PRC's tier-mapping is *response-tier-based* rather than *narrowing-direction-based*.

**Refinement candidate C:** P26 register should be defined by **PRC response-tier mapping** (what severity-tier PRC reaches when responding to this narrowing) rather than by **narrowing-direction lexical mapping** (whether the narrowing is on Taiwan's status or on the response-state's posture). Under candidate C, three-tier register holds across substrates; the narrowing instruments differ but the PRC-tier signature does not.

Two diagnostic markers map cleanly:

2. **中菲建交公报(精神)** — bilateral-founding-document invocation. PHL-side bilateral corpus = 1 document (the 1975-06-09 Joint Communiqué on the Establishment of Diplomatic Relations between PRC and PHL, [Day-11 Source #2]). JPN-side bilateral corpus = 4 documents. **Verdict on PHL substrate Tier 1:** ports cleanly. Same lexical anchor (一个中国原则), bilateral-corpus invocation (one document — the 1975 Establishment Communiqué), advisory-imperative register *不要…玩火*.

**Note:** PHL substrate adds a *register-modulation* marker (advisory-imperative *不要…玩火*) that does not appear on the JPN substrate (which uses the assertive-declarative-condemnation *严重违背* register). Day-11 §3.5 reads this as PRC calibrating the response register to substrate (PHL = junior-tier interlocutor in PRC hierarchy + bilateral-only stakes; JPN = peer-tier interlocutor + alliance-coupled stakes). This is **not** a contradiction of Tier-1 portability but a *within-tier register modulation* axis that future predicate refinement should capture.

### §3.2 — Tier 2 (EU/PHL "policy" medium) — PORTS LEXICALLY EXACTLY ON PHL-DFA

PHL-DFA register, anchored on `publications/2026-05-19-en.md` §3.1 + §3.5 [Day-11 Source #1 + #3]:

> The DFA (Department of Foreign Affairs) under Secretary Enrique Manalo and then Secretary Theresa Lazaro re-stated the formal frame as **"clear and unwavering"** adherence to the **One China Policy** [Source #1, #3]

**One China Policy** verbatim — direct lexical match with the EU-medium tier ("EU 'policy' medium" in the Day-12 P26 reformulation). PHL-DFA occupies Tier 2 with the **exact** EU lexical instrument, in distinct contrast to JPN-MOFA's 1972-Communiqué understand-and-respect formulation.

**Note on register-split:** PHL substrate exhibits a Tier-2 *executive-vs-DFA register split* (P24 active Day-11) where Marcos uses "physical geographic" framing (one notch toward narrowest) and DFA uses "One China Policy" (medium). The split is a within-substrate register-modulation, not a tier-ladder violation — both Marcos and DFA sit at or below the EU-medium tier (neither at PRC maximalist), with DFA at the canonical medium tier and Marcos at a more-narrowing-than-medium-but-not-quite-EP-tier register.

**Verdict on PHL substrate Tier 2:** ports lexically exactly. PHL-DFA "One China Policy" is the canonical EU-medium-tier instrument transplanted to a non-EU substrate. This is the strongest portability result in the test.

### §3.3 — Tier 3 (EP narrowest) — NO ANCHORED EVIDENCE IN DAY-11 CORPUS

Day-11 primary corpus does **not** anchor a PHL-Senate or PHL-House-of-Representatives equivalent of the EP TA-9-2021-0431 rename resolution. The Day-11 publication §3.5 references PHL Senate-side instruments only in the context of the **US MDT Article IV interpretive instruments** (Pompeo 2019 + Blinken 2021/2023 reinterpretations, which are *US executive* not *PHL Senate*); these are not Taiwan-status-narrowing instruments on the PHL side.

The closest PHL-side narrowing instrument in Day-11 corpus is **Marcos 2025-08-11/13 "physical geographic" framing** (Taiwan as a geographic place separable from China-political-status arguments) — but Marcos is *executive*, not legislative, so this does not fill the EP-tier slot per the institutional-source-class criterion. Furthermore, Marcos retracted/clarified within 48 hours via DFA reaffirmation, weakening the narrowing-commitment signal.

**Verdict on PHL substrate Tier 3:** **NO ANCHORED EVIDENCE** in Day-11 corpus. Three possible interpretations:

1. **Real absence**: PHL institutional architecture lacks an EP-equivalent narrowest-tier legislative instrument on Taiwan's status. The Philippine Senate has not passed a Taiwan-status resolution comparable to EP TA-9-2021-0431.
2. **Coverage gap**: Day-11 primary corpus did not seek PHL-Senate-side narrowing instruments because q-A1-A5 fires focused on executive + DFA + AMTI + ZH-primary. A targeted PHL-Senate corpus pull on Day-13+ could surface candidates (SBN bills on PHL-ROC relations 2019-2026; House resolutions on Taiwan disaster-relief / migrant-worker remittance / fishing-boat incidents).
3. **Substrate-specific absence**: PHL's MDT-anchored security architecture makes Taiwan-status narrowing a US-Senate question, not a PHL-Senate question, displacing the institutional slot to a third-party legislature.

Without additional Day-13+ corpus work, the test result for PHL Tier 3 is **inconclusive**, leaning toward (1)+(3) as the structural reading.

## §5 — Refinement options for Day-13 §4 GWW3 framing

Three candidate refinements emerge from the portability test:

### Refinement A (minimum-change, recommended for Day-13 EN-LOCK target)

Keep three-tier register; add lexical-variation annotation at Tier 2:

> Tier 2 (medium): "policy" lexicon (EU + PHL-DFA) **OR** bilateral founding-instrument formula (JPN-MOFA 1972-Communiqué understand-and-respect + N-document spirit invocation). Structurally medium register; instrument depends on substrate institutional convention.

Cost: text-only adjustment; preserves predicate-class continuity from Day-12.

### Refinement B (medium-change)

Split Tier 2 into Tier 2a (policy-lexicon) + Tier 2b (Communiqué-spirit lexicon). Tiers become four-class: PRC maximalist / medium-policy / medium-Communiqué / EP narrowest.

Cost: predicate-class restructure; loses parsimony; requires Day-13 §4 GWW3 decision-table re-numbering. Benefit: lexically explicit; future substrate-pulls (ROK, JPN-pre-PRC-recognition Constitutional position) can classify into the precise sub-tier.

### Refinement C (architectural-change, recommended for Day-14+ if substrate evidence accumulates)

Redefine P26 by **PRC response-tier mapping** rather than **lexical instrument tier**. The register-class is determined by *what severity-tier PRC reaches when responding to the substrate-side instrument*, not by *what lexical instrument the substrate side uses*. Under Refinement C:

- Tier 1 (PRC maximalist) = PRC reaches "一个中国原则 violation + 极其恶劣" or equivalent top-tier severity
- Tier 2 (PRC medium) = PRC reaches "停止错误言行 + 严正立场" or equivalent admonitory register
- Tier 3 (PRC narrowest-acknowledgment) = PRC reaches "中方注意到" / silent / no-response — substrate-side narrowing too narrow to provoke top-tier response

This recasts P26 as a *PRC-response-class* predicate rather than a *substrate-instrument-class* predicate, which has the advantage of being substrate-independent and the disadvantage of requiring a categorisation of every observed PRC response.

**Recommended for Day-13 §4 GWW3 framing: Refinement A** (lexical-variation annotation at Tier 2) as the parsimonious immediate fix that preserves Day-12 predicate continuity. Refinement C tracked as Day-14+ candidate.

# UK Parliament Hansard Taiwan Catalogue (2017-2026)

## §1 Methodology + Scope
This report catalogues the presence of "Tier-3" framing concerning Taiwan's status within the UK Parliament (House of Commons and House of Lords) between 2017 and 2026. The search targeted plenary-level instruments—motions, Early Day Motions (EDMs), and plenary debates—to identify assertions of declarative status narrowing (e.g., "Taiwan is an independent country", "qualifications for statehood", "embassy", "recognition"). The goal is to determine if the Tier-3 register established in the 2023 Foreign Affairs Committee (FAC) report (HC 172) extends to the plenary resolution register or remains isolated to committee and backbench speech.

## §3 Tier-3 Framing Instances (Verbatim)
While no formal resolutions passed with Tier-3 framing, individual members frequently utilized Tier-3 language during plenary debates:

- **16 March 2026 (Lords):** A Lord explicitly quoted the 2023 FAC report on the floor: "...[as the committee] did in 2023 when it declared that Taiwan is already an independent country under the name 'Republic of China' and it possesses all the qualifications for statehood."
- **20 October 2021 (Commons):** A Conservative MP described the diplomatic presence using state-level terms: "Some of us Conservative MPs enjoy regular meetings with the Taiwanese ambassador at his embassy, where we listen to Taiwan's perspective..."
- **17 September 2020 (Lords):** A Lord asked the Government to "make it clear that Taiwan is an independent country and not part of the People's Republic of China."
- **10 February 2022 (Commons):** MPs explicitly called for "Taiwan to have greater international recognition..."

## §4 Null-Findings
An exhaustive search of Early Day Motions (EDMs) and formal plenary resolutions from 2017 to 2026 yielded **zero** instances of Tier-3 framing ("independent country", "qualifications for statehood", "embassy", "recognition"). All Tier-3 lexical occurrences were constrained to backbench speech during debates. The plenary resolution register remains strictly Tier-2.

## §5 Lords + Sub-Committee Parallels
The 16 March 2026 Lords debate demonstrates cross-pollination of the Tier-3 language from the Commons FAC (HC 172) into the House of Lords plenary debate. The specific phrasing ("already an independent country... possesses all the qualifications for statehood") was cited approvingly by Lords. However, there is no evidence of a Lords select committee independently originating a parallel Tier-3 doctrinal statement during this period; they rely on the Commons FAC precedent.

## §6 Implications for P28 Promotion
The evidence confirms that the select-committee register (FAC HC 172) is the "floor" or highest level of institutional output that bears Tier-3 framing. The UK plenary-resolution register (passed motions, EDMs) has not adopted this language, and the Government routinely uses plenary debates to repudiate Tier-3 assertions and reaffirm the Tier-2 "acknowledge" position (e.g., 2019, 2024). Therefore, for P28(c) promotion, we must conclude that while parliamentary *sentiment* (speech) heavily leans into Tier-3, the structural output (resolutions) has not crossed the threshold. The select-committee document remains a positive anomaly rather than a baseline for the plenary.

## §3 — Analytical frame for what the lexical-history finding means

### §3.1 — JPN "四个政治文件精神" invocation-frequency taxonomy

The lexical history measurement should classify each invocation along three axes:

1. **Speaker tier:** MOFA spokesperson (routine register) / MOFA Vice-FM (formal-summons register) / State Council (apex-political register) / MoD (military-doctrinal register) / TAO (cross-strait-as-internal register) / Embassy (foreign-public-diplomacy register)
2. **Co-marker severity:** does the same statement carry top-tier severity vocabulary (极其恶劣 / 玩火者必自焚 / 必遭迎头痛击 / 重蹈军国主义覆辙) or routine register (强烈抗议 / 严正立场 / 注意到)?
3. **Substrate trigger:** what JPN-side act prompted the response? (Diet statement / Cabinet decision / MOFA Blue Book / TS transit / official visit / commercial dispute / etc.)

A "standing-anchor" reading would show "四个政治文件精神" present at most-or-all speaker tiers + most-or-all co-marker severities + most-or-all substrate triggers (a frame invoked across the spectrum). A "graded-severity" reading would show it concentrated at top-tier speaker + top-tier severity + maximalist-substrate-trigger only.

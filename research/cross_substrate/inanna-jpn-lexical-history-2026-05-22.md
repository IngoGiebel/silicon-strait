## 2. Conclusion and Recommendation

**Finding:** The phrase "中日四个政治文件" (Four Political Documents) and its variants have been invoked routinely by PRC-MOFA, State Council apex, and Embassies from at least 2012 to 2026. Crucially, the phrase is used across **all severity levels**—from routine, positive diplomatic framing (e.g., Xi Jinping 2015, Hua Chunying 2012) to top-tier escalations (e.g., responses to Aso/Abe in 2021, and Takaichi in 2025-2026). It is not a recent innovation of the Takaichi-arc, nor is it reserved exclusively for maximalist provocations.

**Recommendation:** These findings confirm **Pattern A** (Standing-anchor lexical instrument). "四个政治文件" is a standing Tier-1 anchor that is always co-present at Tier-1 escalations, but fundamentally serves as a lexical Tier-2 marker for Japan. Therefore, **Refinement A** (keep three-tier register, annotate Tier 2 with lexical-variation note for JPN-MOFA's 1972-Communiqué + 四个政治文件 formula) is the correct framing for the Day-13 §4 GWW3 decision.

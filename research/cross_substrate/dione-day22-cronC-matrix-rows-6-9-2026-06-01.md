**Mao Ning clarification:** India maintains diplomatic relations with China and should *"refrain from doing things that violate the one-China principle."*

### §3.2 Register-tier reading

The Mao Ning June-6 2024 response sits at MOFA-spokesperson level (not Vice-FM, not FM, not summons). Compared to PRC response chains on the JPN Takaichi-2025 substrate (Day-10 §3): MOFA 2025-11-08 → MOFA 2025-11-13 → Vice-FM Sun Weidong summons 2025-11-14 → TAO Chen Binhua 2025-12-10 → MoD Zhang Xiaogang 2026-04-30 — a **5-stage escalation chain**. The India response is a **1-stage MOFA-protest** with no Vice-FM summons, no TAO involvement, no MoD response.

This register-tier asymmetry is itself a substrate-finding. PRC calibrates response severity to:
- (a) the substrate-state's structural distance from PRC's leverage architecture (India higher distance than JPN; India outside US alliance architecture; India holds Sino-Indian boundary-leverage symmetrically);
- (b) the *type* of substrate-state act (rhetorical-Diet-statement under existing alliance vs. reciprocal-head-of-government-congratulation under strategic-autonomy substrate).

The Mao Ning text invokes "serious political commitments" (India-PRC bilateral framework agreements 1949, Panchsheel-1954, Five Principles, etc.) — a *bilateral-doctrinal* register, not a *coercive-toolkit* register (no MOFCOM Notice, no PLA-tempo signal, no economic sanction threat).

Search 2026-06-01 14:13 CEST returned no MOFCOM-Notice-class register specifically targeting Tata-PSMC, HCL-Foxconn, or India-Taiwan industrial coupling. The PRC's industrial-channel response operates through *systemic export-control architecture*:

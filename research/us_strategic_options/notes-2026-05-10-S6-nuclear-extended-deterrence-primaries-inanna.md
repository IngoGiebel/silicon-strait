# Day-7 S6 (Nuclear and extended deterrence) Primary Sources

## 1. DoD 2022 Nuclear Posture Review (NPR)
**Source:** https://media.defense.gov/2022/Oct/27/2003103845/-1/-1/1/2022-NATIONAL-DEFENSE-STRATEGY-NPR-MDR.PDF (Retrieved: 2026-05-10)

**(a) PRC Strategic-Deterrence Assumptions & "Two Near-Peer Competitors" Framing**
> "By the 2030s the United States will, for the first time in its history, face two major nuclear powers as strategic competitors and potential adversaries. This will create new stresses on stability and new challenges for deterrence, assurance, arms control, and risk reduction."

**(b) Taiwan and Extended-Deterrence Guarantees**
*(Note: Taiwan is not extended an explicit "nuclear umbrella" guarantee in the same language used for NATO or formal treaty allies.)*
> "[The United States will] support Taiwan’s asymmetric self-defense commensurate with the evolving PRC threat and consistent with our one China policy."

## 2. Strategic Posture Commission 2023 Final Report
**Source:** https://www.ida.org/-/media/feature/publications/a/am/americas-strategic-posture/strategic-posture-commission-report.pdf (Retrieved: 2026-05-10)

**(a) China-Specific Findings and the "Two-Peer Challenge"**
> "For the first time in its history, the U.S. must deter two nuclear-armed peer adversaries (China and Russia) simultaneously. The current U.S. strategic posture is ill-prepared for this reality."
> "The years 2027–2035 are identified as a high-risk period due to the rapid expansion of China's nuclear forces and Russia's continued modernization."

## 4. DoD 2024 China Military Power Report (CMPR)
**Source:** U.S. Department of Defense (Retrieved summary context via search: 2026-05-10)

**(a) PRC Nuclear Modernization and Projections**
> "As of mid-2024, China’s operational nuclear warhead stockpile has surpassed 600."
> "China is on track to possess over 1,000 operational warheads by 2030."
> "China will likely field approximately 1,500 warheads by 2035."

## §1 — Why AUS is the right Day-15 thread

The Day-14 EN-LOCK §6 named AUS as Q1 and CAN as Q2 of two substrate-extension questions load-bearing for the Day-15+ trajectory, with AUS preferred on three independent grounds:

1. **Substrate-accessibility.** The Parliament of Australia's Hansard archive (aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Hansard) is fully searchable 1981→present with stable URL conventions; JSCFADT (Joint Standing Committee on Foreign Affairs, Defence and Trade) publishes reports and inquiry submissions to aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Committees/Joint/Foreign_Affairs_Defence_and_Trade with stable inquiry-page anchors. Equivalent CAN tooling (openparliament.ca + ourcommons.ca) has comparable depth but a known UI/URL-stability gap on committee reports relative to AUS. AUS-first cuts research-fire latency.
2. **Five-Eyes corner anchor.** AUS is the structural corner of the Five-Eyes namespace that the eventual US-substrate study (Day-17+ thread, deferred since Day-7 US Strategic Options published 2026-05-11) will need as a cross-substrate triangulation point. Choosing AUS at Day-15 lets the Day-17+ US-substrate work cite a pre-existing AUS-substrate finding rather than backfilling it after the fact.
3. **AUKUS proximity.** AUS-PRC tension since the 2020-2021 wine/coal/barley/timber/lobster trade-pressure cycle through the 2021-09 AUKUS announcement through the 2022 Albanese-Wong stabilisation cycle through the 2023-08-12 lifting of PRC wine tariffs and the 2024-onward stabilisation provides a *richer* PRC-relationship-shock-and-recovery substrate than CAN's Meng/Two-Michaels arc, which is structurally narrower (single hostage-diplomacy event + Foreign Interference Commission inquiry). The thicker AUS-PRC instrument record supplies more Tier-3-instrument candidates per unit research-fire-time.

The risk on the AUS choice is that **AUS lacks a verbatim 1972-style Joint Communiqué historical anchor in the UK shape** — AUS-PRC diplomatic relations were established 1972-12-21 via a separate Whitlam-Government communiqué with its own lexical choices on Taiwan-status (§3 q-day15-3 below tests whether that lexical baseline parallels or diverges from UK 1972). If the Whitlam communiqué turns out structurally distinct from the UK 1972 mixed-register opening, the AUS-substrate test still proceeds — it just no longer rests on a paired 1972-anchored lexical-historical foundation, and the P28 substrate-discriminating refinement must rest entirely on the parliamentary-instrument-tier evidence (JSCFADT register, Senate FADTRC register, Chamber + Senate Hansard debates). That is a known structural feature of the AUS choice, not a failure mode.

## §5 — Eight-pruning candidate (Day-15 D15.x)

- **D15.1 AUS dis-establishes the 1972 Whitlam-Government Joint Communiqué framework with the PRC.** Deterministically blocked by:
  - The Whitlam 1972 communiqué is the *legal-historical foundation* of AUS-PRC diplomatic relations; dis-establishment requires either a successor instrument or a formal de-recognition act. AUS has had multiple Government rotations since 1972 (Whitlam→Fraser→Hawke→Keating→Howard→Rudd→Gillard→Rudd→Abbott→Turnbull→Morrison→Albanese) and none have produced a successor or de-recognition instrument; the structural cost of doing so during the most-strained 2020-2021 PRC trade-pressure cycle was already revealed-preference-rejected.
  - AUS-PRC trade exposure: PRC was AUS's largest two-way trading partner 2022 calendar-year; goods + services 2-way ~AUD 280-300bn class. Dis-establishment triggers an export-substitution problem with multi-year adjustment horizon, against an AUS economy whose Treasury and Reserve Bank planning frameworks have no pre-committed substitution-path the way Lithuania-EU did in 2021-2022.
  - AUKUS Pillar-I planning horizon: dis-establishment introduces a multi-year US-policy-coordination friction at exactly the moment the AUKUS SSN-AUKUS schedule needs maximum US-side commitment continuity. The Albanese-Wong stabilisation cycle is partly motivated by stabilising the PRC-relationship surface so the AUKUS-implementation surface can absorb scarce diplomatic-and-political-attention bandwidth.
- **D15.2 candidate (subject to research) — AUS formally recognises the ROC.** Same shape as D11.2 (PHL recognises ROC) and D10.1 (JPN re-recognises ROC) but with AUS-specific institutional substrate: AUS-ROC unofficial relations are AOTI (Australian Office Taipei) / TECO Canberra mediated. Verify the deterministic floor before committing D15.2 as eight-prune candidate.

## Source 1 — CSIS ChinaPower: "What Does China Really Spend on its Military?"

**Published:** 2015-12-28; **Updated:** 2026-03-05
**Authors:** China Power Team
**URL:** https://chinapower.csis.org/military-spending/

### Headline numbers

| Year | Official budget | USD equiv | YoY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | (not extracted) | ~$231 B | — |
| 2025 | 1.78 trillion RMB | $244.8 B | +7% |
| 2026 | 1.91 trillion RMB | $276.7 B | upper-single-digit |

### Outside-analyst estimates of "true" spend

- **SIPRI 2024:** $313.7 B
- **IISS 2024:** $325 B
- **PPP-adjusted (2024 study):** ~$471 B (sector-specific PPP)
- **US DoD:** actual spending may be 32–63% higher than officially stated

### % of GDP

- China: "at or below 2 percent of GDP since 2000"
- US comparison: ~3.8% over the same period (so a US-style discipline would imply roughly +90% to PRC defence spending if China matched US-as-share-of-GDP commitment)

### What's outside the budget (transparency caveat)

- Military R&D
- Space programs
- People's Armed Police ($21.5 B in 2024 alone)
- Defense mobilization funds
- Recruitment bonuses
- (Reading: this matches the DoD 32–63% gap)

### Composition

- 2017 white paper: equipment ~41% of total spend
- UN-reported 2022: 36.6%

### GWW3-relevant framing (Dione synthesis)

The numerical opacity is itself an information-war datum. In Belief Subgraph terms, every player has uncertainty over PRC's true military spend; the spread between official ($244.8 B) and PPP-adjusted ($471 B) is roughly 1.92x — a credible-uncertainty range that bilateral signaling games will exploit. **A model that uses one point estimate is wrong by construction.**

## Source 2 — CSIS ChinaPower: "Tracking China's Increased Military Activities in the Indo-Pacific in 2025"

**Published:** 2026-02-05
**Authors:** Bonny Lin, Brian Hart, Leon Li, Truly Tinsley
**URL:** https://chinapower.csis.org/china-increased-military-activities-indo-pacific-2025/

### Taiwan ADIZ / Strait crossings (2025)

- **3,764 ADIZ incursions in 2025**, +22.4% vs 2024
- **May 2024 – Dec 2025:** average 319 monthly aircraft sorties (+129% vs Jan 2022 – Apr 2024 baseline of ~156/mo) — **step-change in late spring 2024**, not gradual ramp
- **Naval vessels:** 221/month average in 2025, +42% vs Aug 2022 – Apr 2024 (156/mo); **190+/month sustained baseline since May 2024**
- **YoY decline May–Dec window:** 2,479 (2025) vs 2,613 (2024) = -5.4% — the *trend* is decelerating in absolute count even as the year-total grows

### Major exercises

| Exercise | Date | Air | Naval |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strait Thunder-2025A** | Apr 2025 | 135 sorties | 38 vessels |
| **Justice Mission-2025** | Dec 29-30, 2025 | 8 exercise zones | 27 rockets launched |
| Joint Sword-2024B (comparator) | Oct 2024 | 153 sorties | — |

### Beyond Taiwan

- **South China Sea:** 163 PLA operations in 2025 (record); 55 live-fire (vs 47 in 2024)
- **CCG presence at Scarborough Shoal:** "more than doubled" 2024→2025
- **Japan/Senkaku:** 1,380 CCG/state vessels in Senkaku contiguous zone in 2025 (record); territorial-sea entries 89 (2025) vs 115 (2024) = -23%
- **PLAN voyages near Japan:** 111 (2025) vs 108 (2024) — flat
- **Beyond First Island Chain:** carriers operated **58 days outside FIC in 2025** vs 32 in 2024; **carrier sorties 1,680 in 2025 vs 1,240 in 2024** = +35.5%
- **China-Russia joint exercises:** 6 in 2025 (lowest since 2020; down from 14 in 2024) — **noteworthy decoupling signal**

### Cited primary sources

- Taiwan Ministry of National Defense
- Japan Ministry of Defense
- China Maritime Safety Administration
- CSIS Asia Maritime Transparency Initiative (AMTI)
- Open-source vessel tracking

### GWW3-relevant framing (Dione synthesis)

Three structural patterns from the 2025 data:

1. **Step-change (not ramp) at May 2024.** ADIZ baseline jumped from ~156 to ~319 sorties/month — coincident with Lai inauguration (May 20, 2024). This is a *signaling* move, not capacity growth. Belief Subgraph: PRC raised the "expected harassment level" floor to communicate that a friendly-to-Taiwan US/Japan posture is now structurally costly.

2. **Quality over quantity in late 2025.** YoY ADIZ count declines (-5.4% May–Dec window) while exercises grow in scale (Strait Thunder-2025A, Justice Mission-2025). Schelling-game read: PRC is moving from "pressure" mode to "rehearsal" mode. The shift in mix matters more than the headline 22.4% annual figure.

3. **Carrier ops outside First Island Chain +81% in days, +35% in sorties.** This is the most A2/AD-relevant 2025 data point: PRC is no longer staying inside the bubble, it's operating beyond it. *The Silicon Shield assumption that PRC operates inside a defensive perimeter is being eroded.*

4. **China-Russia joint exercises down 57% (14 → 6).** Decoupling signal worth investigating in a later thread (`russia_spoiler`); for Day-4 it's a footnote that the "China-Russia bloc" framing is weaker in 2025 than in 2024.

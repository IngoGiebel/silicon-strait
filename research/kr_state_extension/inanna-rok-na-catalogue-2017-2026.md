## Summary & Register Classification

### Classification Recommendation: Tier-1 / Low Tier-2 Ceiling
Based on the P28 advancement-ceiling framework, the ROK National Assembly exhibits a **Tier-1 / Low Tier-2 ceiling**.

- **Supporting Evidence Count:** 20 distinct instruments/events identified (Resolutions: 4, Floor Speeches: 7, Committee Hearings/Minutes: 8, Joint Statements: 1).
- **Register Comparison:**
    - Unlike the **US (Tier-3/4)**, there are no enacted laws (like the TRA or TAIPEI Act) structurally narrowing Taiwan's status or mandating diplomatic elevation.
    - Unlike **CAN (Tier-3)**, there are no bills advancing through readings that explicitly define Taiwan as a foreign state.
    - The ROK register aligns most closely with the **UK and AUS (Tier-1)**, where activity is confined to committee reports, hearing discussions, floor speeches, and non-binding parliamentary motions (e.g., EDMs in the UK, WHA resolutions in ROK).
    - The ROK activity displays a slight Tier-2 character due to the formal submission of resolutions (WHA participation), analogous to EP non-binding resolutions, but these are frequently left pending or expire without plenary passage.

### Analytical Observations
1. **Thematic Clustering:** ROK legislative activity concerning Taiwan is highly clustered around non-traditional security (global health/WHA) and supply-chain economics (semiconductors). Hard-security status-narrowing instruments are absent.
2. **"Global Issue" Shift:** Post-2021 (and particularly post-2023 following the "global issue" presidential framing), FAUC hearings and floor speeches increasingly contextualize Taiwan Strait stability as a core ROK national interest, rather than merely an inter-Korean parallel.
3. **Parliamentary Diplomacy as Proxy:** The Korea-Taiwan Parliamentarian Friendship Association operates as a critical proxy vehicle. Because formal statutory advancement is constrained by the 1992 PRC-ROK Communiqué architecture, lawmakers use high-profile visits and consequent PRC diplomatic friction as the primary mechanism for expressing Tier-2 support.

## Source 1 — 2024-05-20 Inaugural Address

**Citation:** Office of the President, Republic of China (Taiwan). (2024). *Inaugural Address of ROC 16th-term President Lai Ching-te*. [https://english.president.gov.tw/Page/651](https://english.president.gov.tw/Page/651) (Language: EN; original delivered in ZH 2024-05-20)

### Load-bearing verbatim passages

**Constitutional sovereignty frame:**
> "The sovereignty of the Republic of China shall reside in the whole body of citizens."
> "The Republic of China and the People's Republic of China are not subordinate to each other."

**Call to PRC (dialogue framing):**
> Lai urged China to "face the reality of the Republic of China's existence, respect the choices of the people of Taiwan" and "choose dialogue over confrontation, exchange over containment."

**Defense / deterrence anchor (the single most-cited paragraph for the deterrence-realist reading of Lai):**
> "So long as China refuses to renounce the use of force against Taiwan, all of us in Taiwan ought to understand, that even if we accept the entirety of China's position and give up our sovereignty, China's ambition to annex Taiwan will not simply disappear."

### The Four Pillars of Peace

Lai's framework, outlined in the address:
1. Strengthened national defense
2. Improved economic security
3. Stable cross-strait leadership
4. Values-based diplomacy

Aggregating tag: **"peace through strength."**

### Analytical pointers

- The "not subordinate to each other" formulation directly continues Tsai Ing-wen's 2021 National Day phrasing — Lai is explicitly anchoring continuity, not break. (For Tsai-baseline cross-check, see § future-fire research.)
- The "even if we accept the entirety of China's position" passage is a deterministic argument-from-PRC-revealed-preferences: it functions as a logical proof, not just an emotional appeal. This makes it suitable for a GWW3 predicate.
- "Peace through strength" is a Reagan-era US slogan; its adoption by Lai signals a deliberate ideational alignment with US-Republican defense frames (especially Trump-2 era — relevant for the Day-7 S7 US-domestic-politics carry-over).

## Source 2 — 2026-01-01 New Year's Address

**Citation:** Office of the President, Republic of China (Taiwan). (2026). *President Lai delivers 2026 New Year's Address*. [https://english.president.gov.tw/NEWS/7067](https://english.president.gov.tw/NEWS/7067) (Language: EN; original delivered in ZH 2026-01-01)

### Load-bearing verbatim passages

**On PRC threat framing (escalated rhetoric vs. inaugural):**
> "China's rising expansionary ambitions"
> "China's alarming military ambitions"
> "Facing China's alarming military ambitions, Taiwan has no time to wait and certainly no time to consume ourselves with internal strife."

**Defense budget (single most-cited 2026 number for ROC defense planning):**
> "eight-year, NT$1.25 trillion special defense budget to comprehensively upgrade our combat capabilities and strengthen our defense industry"

**Doctrinal language:**
> "strengthen national defense, and enhance whole-of-society defense and resilience, and to comprehensively establish robust deterrence and democratic defense mechanisms."

**On US-Taiwan and allied alignment:**
> "The US recently announced its largest-ever military sale to Taiwan, as well as highlighted Taiwan's importance in its National Security Strategy."
> "Countries including the United States, Japan, the United Kingdom, and European Union nations have all expressed support for Taiwan."

**On bipartisan defense-budget passage (the structural plea):**
> "I hope the ruling and opposition parties can work together to swiftly pass this crucial national defense budget."
> "in the new year, the ruling and opposition parties can work together, unite the nation in the face of both internal and external challenges, and begin a new chapter."

**Economic indicators (anchoring resilience narrative):**
> "economic growth rate has hit a 15-year high and is projected to reach 7.37 percent"
> "unemployment rate averaged around 3 percent, and employment is in its best condition in 25 years"
> "Taiwan Stock Index set new records"

### Analytical pointers

- **Rhetorical escalation from 2024 → 2026.** The inaugural said "renounce the use of force"; the NY Address says "alarming military ambitions" and "rising expansionary ambitions." That's a moved baseline — Lai is no longer requesting Chinese de-escalation, he's coding it as out of reach and framing the response as accelerated defense buildup.
- **NT$1.25T / 8 years = NT$156.25B/year average.** Taiwan's 2025 defense budget was ~NT$647B (~2.45% GDP). The new package, if additive (special budget, not absorbed into baseline), pushes Taiwan toward ~3% GDP defense spending — matching the threshold the Trump-2 administration has publicly demanded of Taiwan. This is the **S4 defense-reform anchor**.
- **"Ruling and opposition parties" framing repeated twice in one address.** Confirms my earlier hypothesis (Insight 1 above): Lai's executive position is structurally weak on budget — DPP is minority in the Legislative Yuan post-Jan 2024 election. Defense package passage is not assured; it requires KMT and TPP cooperation. This is the **S2 KMT opposition gating mechanism** for the GWW3 ROC-player model.
- **Economic numbers are 2025 actuals.** 7.37% growth is the highest in 15 years and reflects the AI/semiconductor capex boom — and almost certainly TSMC's revenue gravity. This is the **S5 economic-resilience anchor** and also the link back to chip-supply-chain Stream-2.

## Source 3 — 2026-02-03 Press Conference: Taiwan-US Economic Prosperity Partnership Dialogue (EPPD)

**Citation:** Office of the President, Republic of China (Taiwan). (2026). *President Lai holds press conference on Taiwan-US Economic Prosperity Partnership Dialogue*. [https://english.president.gov.tw/NEWS/7082](https://english.president.gov.tw/NEWS/7082) (Language: EN; original ZH 2026-02-03)

### Load-bearing verbatim passages

**Mechanism origin (anchors Trump-1 continuity claim, relevant for Day-7 S7 carry-over):**
> "Last week, the sixth annual Taiwan-US EPPD was held in Washington, DC. This mechanism was established during US President Donald Trump's first term."

**Four pillars of the dialogue (2026 round):**
> "This round of the dialogue focused on four key pillars: strategic alignment on supply chain security, cooperation on critical minerals, cooperation in third countries, and bilateral cooperation."

**Three strategic directions:**
> "enhancing economic security, building up an innovation-driven economy, and fostering a prosperous future"

**On semiconductor framing:**
> "AI, semiconductor, and other high-tech industries are placing increasing emphasis on security, trustworthiness, and resilience."

**Legislative plea (parallel to NY Address structure):**
> "I sincerely hope that, in their deliberation on the Taiwan-US tariff agreement, members of the Legislative Yuan will uphold professionalism, proceed in accordance with the law."

### Analytical pointers

- **EPPD was Trump-1 vintage and survived through Biden-1 and into Trump-2.** This is a stability anchor in an otherwise volatile US partisanship environment. Lai's deliberate citation of "Trump's first term" is a coalition-management signal aimed at US Republicans.
- **"Critical minerals" entering as a 2026 pillar.** New since pre-2026 versions of the dialogue — reflects the post-2025 expansion of US economic-security framing beyond chips. Taiwan has no major critical-minerals reserves; the cooperation framing is probably about third-country processing partnerships (Australia? Indonesia?). Worth a follow-up research note.
- **Tariff-agreement legislative plea = second parallel to the NY Address.** Same structural problem: executive negotiates with US, legislature must ratify, opposition controls legislature. Whatever tariff package Lai brings home from Washington requires the same KMT-TPP cooperation as the defense budget. This **doubles** the S2 KMT-opposition gating mechanism — it's not just defense, it's the entire US-Taiwan economic policy stack.

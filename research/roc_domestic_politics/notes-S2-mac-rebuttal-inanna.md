### Verbatim Load-Bearing Passages

> "The Mainland Affairs Council (MAC) stated today (February 3) that the crux of the current cross-Strait situation lies in the Chinese Communist Party’s (CCP) continued escalation of pressure against Taiwan and its growing hostility... The Kuomintang (KMT), however, has turned a blind eye to the CCP’s intent to eliminate the Republic of China and annex Taiwan, and has insisted on co-hosting a “KMT-CCP Think Tank Forum” with the CCP." [^1]

> "The two sides further released “Joint Declarations” under the political premise of the “1992 Consensus” and opposition to “Taiwan independence,” falsely claiming that they would promote the lifting of restrictions imposed by Taiwan’s government on cross-Strait people-to-people exchanges. This narrative completely misattributes responsibility for the suspension of exchanges and is categorically rejected by the government." [^1]

> "The MAC emphasized that the artificial barriers preventing normal cross-Strait people-to-people exchanges are entirely created by the CCP. Mainland Chinese tourism to Taiwan has been suspended for many years, and the cross-Strait situation has undergone significant changes..." [^1]

> "The MAC emphasized that the KMT-CCP “Joint Declarations” align with the CCP’s policies of integrating Taiwan into its system and conducting united front operations, and represent a reversion to the old approach of westward orientation and reliance on the CCP." [^1]

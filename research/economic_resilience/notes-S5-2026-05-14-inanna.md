## Thread 1: MOEA energy security
**Source:** Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA) Press Release (2025-10-25)
**URL:** https://www.moea.gov.tw/Mns/populace/news/News.aspx?kind=1&menu_id=40&news_id=120882
**Access Date:** 2026-05-14

**Natural Gas Inventory & Import Diversification:**
> "經濟部指出，因應未來天然氣占比增加，政府已訂定天然氣安全存量規範，並規劃自現行11天逐步提升至2027年14天以上，同時透過來源多元化與中長期契約等確保進口穩定，並配合再生能源與儲能發展，強化系統在極端氣候或突發情況下的韌性與調節能力，以降低能源供應風險。"
*Translation:* The MOEA noted that in response to the future increase in the proportion of natural gas, the government has established natural gas safety inventory regulations, planning to gradually increase the current 11 days to 14 days or more by 2027. At the same time, it ensures import stability through source diversification and medium-to-long-term contracts, and alongside the development of renewable energy and energy storage, strengthens system resilience and regulation capability under extreme weather or emergency situations to reduce energy supply risks.

**Electricity-Grid Resilience:**
> "台電公司2022年啟動10年5645億元「強化電網韌性建設計畫」，以力求分散、持續強固、加強防衛3大主軸，推動現行電網系統升級工程，強化特高壓電網，以逐步達到電網分散，提高設備穩定程度，提升全國電網面對突發事故的因應能力。"
*Translation:* Taipower launched a 10-year, NT$564.5 billion "Grid Resilience Construction Plan" in 2022, focusing on three main axes: decentralization, continuous strengthening, and enhanced defense. It promotes the upgrading of the current grid system and strengthens the extra-high voltage grid to gradually achieve grid decentralization, improve equipment stability, and enhance the national grid's response capability to emergencies.

## Thread 3: Submarine-cable redundancy
**Source:** Chunghwa Telecom (CHT) Press Release (2026-04-16)
**URL:** https://www.cht.com.tw/home/enterprise/news/success-case/2026/20260416_1
**Access Date:** 2026-05-14

**Multi-Orbit Satellite Backup (OneWeb / SES O3b / Astranis):**
> "林榮賜表示，中華電信作為台灣電信領導品牌，為強化國家與城市韌性，近年更積極布局多軌道衛星，除既有高軌衛星ST-2，並於去年陸續取得低軌衛星OneWeb與中軌衛星SES O3b商用許可，是國內唯一擁有完整高、中、低多軌道衛星群的業者，並預計2026年底前與美國Astranis衛星公司策略合作，發射台灣首顆專屬衛星，持續擴充多軌道衛星頻寬與備援彈性。"
*Translation:* Lin Jung-szu stated that Chunghwa Telecom, as Taiwan's leading telecom brand, has actively deployed multi-orbit satellites in recent years to strengthen national and urban resilience. In addition to the existing ST-2 geostationary satellite, it obtained commercial licenses for the OneWeb low-earth orbit (LEO) satellite and SES O3b medium-earth orbit (MEO) satellite last year. It is the only operator in Taiwan with a complete GEO, MEO, and LEO satellite fleet, and it expects to partner strategically with the US satellite company Astranis to launch Taiwan's first dedicated satellite by the end of 2026, continuing to expand multi-orbit satellite bandwidth and backup flexibility.

**"Sea, Land, and Starry Sky" (海地星空) Multi-Layered Resilience:**
> "這套韌性架構設計也形成中華電信近年的「海地星空」布局，涵蓋海纜、光纖與行動通訊、微波及衛星，打造互為備援、多層次的韌性網路。海纜為台灣對外通訊主要核心，並透過多路由配置提升整體可靠度；島內則由光纖、行動網路與微波共同支撐地面網路。衛星則作為極端情境下的重要備援，並補足山區、離島或災後搶修期間的通訊需求。"
*Translation:* This resilient architectural design also shapes Chunghwa Telecom's recent "Sea, Land, and Starry Sky" layout, covering submarine cables, optical fiber, mobile communications, microwave, and satellites to create a mutually redundant, multi-layered resilient network. Submarine cables are the main core of Taiwan's external communications, with overall reliability enhanced through multi-route configurations; domestically, optical fiber, mobile networks, and microwaves jointly support the terrestrial network. Satellites serve as a critical backup under extreme scenarios and fulfill communication needs in mountainous areas, outlying islands, or during post-disaster repair periods.

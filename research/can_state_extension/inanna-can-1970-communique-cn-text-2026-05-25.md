# CAN-PRC 1970 Communiqué CN-Text Verification
**Date:** 2026-05-25
**Analyst:** Inanna

## Findings
The official Chinese text of the October 13, 1970 Joint Communiqué between Canada and the PRC (中华人民共和国政府和加拿大政府关于中、加两国建立外交关系的联合公报) uses the following lexical formulation:

> 中国政府重申：台湾是中华人民共和国领土不可分割的一部分。加拿大政府**注意到**中国政府的这一立场。
> 加拿大政府**承认**中华人民共和国政府为中国的唯一合法政府。

## Analysis
- **"Takes note" translation:** The English phrase "The Canadian Government takes note of this position" is translated in the official Chinese text as **注意到** (zhùyì dào).
- **"Recognizes" translation:** The English phrase "recognizes the Government of the PRC" is translated as **承认** (chéngrèn).

**Conclusion:** Unlike the 1972 UK and AUS communiqués where "acknowledges" and "recognizes" were collapsed into the same Chinese character (承认) by the PRC, the 1970 Canadian communiqué preserves the lexical distinction in the CN-text layer. The Canadian "takes note" (注意到) is linguistically distinct from "recognizes" (承认). This confirms that Canada's formulation sits at a different, hierarchy-preserving lexical floor compared to the UK/AUS collapsed-asymmetry.
